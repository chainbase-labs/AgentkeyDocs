WeChat Official Accounts (公众号) are where Chinese media, brands and
independent writers publish long-form, and the content is famously unsearchable
from outside WeChat: there is no public API, no web index and no way to see an
article's read count without the app. Through AgentKey your agent reads public
Official Account data the same way it reads everything else — `find_tools`,
`describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — with several providers behind the scenes, so
one route failing does not stop the task.

## What you can ask

- *"Pull the full text of this WeChat article and summarize it."*
- *"这篇公众号文章的阅读量和点赞数是多少？"*
- *"List the recent articles from the 人民日报 official account."*
- *"Which original articles went viral yesterday?"*
- *"Search WeChat articles about AI search from the past week."*
- *"Who operates this official account — the registered principal and verification?"*
- *"How has the WeChat Index for 新能源汽车 moved over the past month?"*
- *"Which accounts gained the most readers recently?"*

## What's covered

**Articles**
- Article detail by URL or by UUID, as plain text or rich text, with the full
  body
- Read, like and other interaction metrics; article stats
- Comments on an article and the replies under a comment
- Ads shown inside an article; related articles
- Expansion of a short or intermediate article link to its resolved URL

**Accounts**
- Account info by name, wxid or biz ID, including principal (ownership)
  information
- Historical articles and the account's article list, paginated
- Original-article count; the services and custom menu an account offers
- Account search by keyword

**Search**
- Article search by keyword with publish-time and sort filters; a search for
  AI-generated articles
- Universal WeChat search; search suggestions scoped by business type
- Mini Program search

**Rankings and trends**
- Original viral article ranking and the 100K+ reads article ranking
- Reading-growth ranking and overall strength ranking of accounts
- WeChat Index for a keyword — interest inside WeChat over time

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="full text and read count of this WeChat official account article")
find_tools(q="这篇公众号文章的阅读量和点赞数")
find_tools(prefix="social/wechat_mp")      # browse Official Account tools
find_tools(prefix="social/wechat")         # more article, account and Index tools
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work, and the platform's usual English and Chinese names all land here. Always `describe_tool` before executing —
parameters and per-call costs differ between providers.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a WeChat account or an Official Account of my own?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh are the metrics and rankings?">
    Article detail, metrics and comments go to WeChat at request time. The
    viral-article and 100K+ reads rankings describe the previous day and are
    refreshed once a day.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What about WeChat Channels videos?">
    Channels (视频号) is a separate surface with its own tools; see
    [WeChat Channels](/capabilities/social/wechat-channels-data-for-ai-agents). One of those tools
    finds the Channels account bound to an Official Account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
