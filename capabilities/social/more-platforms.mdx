Beyond the platforms with their own pages, the Social category covers a long
tail of networks and content sites. Each works the same way — `find_tools`,
`describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — and the browse prefix for each is listed with
its section. These platforms are available through the catalog only; none of
them has fixed-contract Scenario Methods yet.

## Global

### Facebook

Browse with `find_tools(prefix="social/facebook")`.

- Public post search by keyword, with an optional inclusive date range
- A profile's public posts, after resolving the profile ID from its path
- Comments on a post and the replies under a comment

### Telegram

Browse with `find_tools(prefix="social/telegram")`.

- Channel info, single or in batch; similar channels
- A channel's posts, paginated; search for posts inside a channel
- A single post and its comments (discussion)

### Lemon8

Browse with `find_tools(prefix="social/lemon8")`.

- Discover page — banners, main content and Editor's Picks; hot search keywords
- Keyword search; post info and the comments on a post
- Topic info and the posts under a topic
- User info, fans and following; post and user IDs from sharing links, single or
  batch

### IMDb

Browse with `find_tools(prefix="social/imdb")`.

- Title lookup by ID — base info, details and extended details, plot, top cast
  and crew, countries of origin, release expectation
- Awards, box office, critics-review and user-review summaries, "Did You Know"
  trivia, recommendations of similar titles
- Top 250 movie and TV charts; news by category; Prime Video streaming picks
- Language and country preferences on every call

## China

### Toutiao (今日头条)

Browse with `find_tools(prefix="social/toutiao")`.

- Article detail and video detail; comments on a post
- User profile, user ID from a profile URL, and a user's works
- Keyword search for articles and works, and for accounts

### Xigua Video (西瓜视频)

Browse with `find_tools(prefix="social/xigua")`.

- Video detail and the video play URL
- User info and a user's posts
- Video comments; video search

### Pipixia (皮皮虾)

Browse with `find_tools(prefix="social/pipixia")`.

- Home feed and the short-drama feed; hot search words and hot search boards
- Hashtag detail and the posts under a hashtag; keyword search
- Post detail, statistics and comments
- User info, posts, followers and following

### Douban (豆瓣)

Browse with `find_tools(prefix="social/douban")`.

- Movie or TV subject detail
- Short comments ordered by time or newest rating; long-form reviews and a
  single review's detail
- Recent hot movies and recent hot TV

### QQ Huxuan (互选)

Browse with `find_tools(prefix="social/qq-huxuan")`. Huxuan is Tencent's
brand-creator matching platform for Channels and Official Accounts.

- Video Account creators — search by nickname, creator detail, recent videos
  within a date range
- Official Account creators — search by nickname or account, creator detail,
  article list within a date range with an optional title keyword
- Monthly hot-content rankings for Video Account creators by category, sorted
  by plays, likes, interactions and other metrics

### Youku (优酷)

Browse with `find_tools(prefix="social/youku")`.

- Video search by keyword
- Video detail; user profile

### Yiche (易车)

Browse with `find_tools(prefix="social/yiche")`.

- Article detail and video detail
- A user's works; account search; works search by keyword

### Autohome (汽车之家)

Browse with `find_tools(prefix="social/autohome")`.

- Chejiahao (车家号) article detail and video detail
- An author's works; works search by keyword

### Dongchedi (懂车帝)

Browse with `find_tools(prefix="social/dongchedi")`.

- Work detail
- A user's works; user search; works search by keyword

## How to use them

```text
find_tools(q="recent posts in this Telegram channel")
find_tools(q="豆瓣上这部电影的短评")
find_tools(prefix="social/imdb")           # browse one platform
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work. Always `describe_tool` before executing
— parameters and per-call costs differ between tools.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** These platforms are available through the catalog
  tools only; there are no fixed-contract Scenario Methods for them yet. See
  the [Scenario Methods overview](/api-reference/scenario/overview) for the
  platforms that have them.
</Note>
