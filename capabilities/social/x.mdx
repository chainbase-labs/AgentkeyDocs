X is where news breaks and opinions form, and it is one of the hardest
platforms for an agent to reach: the official API is expensive and the website
blocks scraping. Through AgentKey your agent reads public X data the same way it
reads everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — with
several providers behind the scenes, so one route failing does not stop the
task.

## What you can ask

- *"What are people saying about AgentKey on X today?"*
- *"Pull the last 20 posts from @NASA and summarize the themes."*
- *"Show me the replies and quote tweets on this post, with the overall sentiment."*
- *"Who follows this account, and which of them are verified?"*
- *"Which accounts gained the most followers in the past week among these ten?"*
- *"What is trending on X in Japan right now?"*
- *"Is this account shadowbanned?"*
- *"List the members of this X community and what they have been posting."*

## What's covered

**Posts**
- Keyword search with X's operators — `from:`, `to:`, `since:` / `until:`,
  exact phrases, hashtags, media filters
- Post detail by ID or URL, in single and batch form
- Replies and comments, quote posts, the list of reposters
- Long-form articles, a user's media posts, a user's latest replies

**Users**
- Profile by handle or ID, single or batch; the "about" page; affiliates
- Followers and following, verified followers, follower category breakdowns
- New followers and new following over the last seven days
- Handle ↔ ID conversion, profile link → ID, live-status and shadowban checks

**Lists and communities**
- List posts, list members, list followers
- Community info, members and timeline; community search; keyword search
  inside a community; membership checks

**Trends and Spaces**
- Trending topics, by country
- Spaces information

**Relationship and quality signals**
- Does A follow B, did A repost or quote this post
- Follower-quality scores and their changes over time, top followers and top
  following by score

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="what people are saying about AgentKey on X today")
find_tools(q="推特上 @NASA 最近发了什么")
find_tools(prefix="social/twitter")        # browse everything for X
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work, and aliases resolve (推特, Twitter and
X all land here). Always `describe_tool` before executing — parameters and
per-call costs differ between providers.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an X developer account or API key?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it read protected accounts or direct messages?">
    No. Only public posts, profiles and relationships are available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh is the data?">
    Every call goes to the platform at request time; nothing is served from a
    stale archive. Trend and search results reflect what X returns at that
    moment.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
