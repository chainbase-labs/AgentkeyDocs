Xiaohongshu (小红书, RedNote) is where Chinese consumers research purchases,
travel and lifestyle, and it has no public API at all: every note sits behind
app-signed requests and a login wall. Through AgentKey your agent reads public
Xiaohongshu data the same way it reads everything else — `find_tools`,
`describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — with several providers behind the scenes, so
one route failing does not stop the task.

## What you can ask

- *"What are the most-liked notes about sunscreen on Xiaohongshu this month?"*
- *"小红书上关于防晒霜的热门笔记有哪些？"*
- *"Pull this note's text, images and comments and summarize how people react."*
- *"Show me this account's recent notes and their likes, collects and comments."*
- *"Which beauty creators are open for cooperation, and what are their quotes?"*
- *"What is in today's hot-notes ranking, and which dark-horse notes went viral overnight?"*
- *"帮我看看这个博主在蒲公英上的粉丝画像和报价"*
- *"Transcribe this Xiaohongshu video note."*

## What's covered

**Notes**
- Keyword search with sort, note-type and publish-time filters; a search for
  AI-generated notes
- Note detail by ID or URL — body text, watermark-free images or the video play
  URL, hashtags, author and engagement counts
- Short share-link resolution
- Hot-notes insight: the most-engaged notes, with or without a keyword

**Comments**
- First-level comments sorted by default, latest or like count; replies under a
  comment
- Bulk first-level comment pulls as a submit-then-fetch task

**Users**
- Profile by user ID or profile URL — bio, IP location, follower, like and
  collect counts
- Published notes; collected (faved) notes
- User search by nickname or niche; account info

**Topics and discovery**
- Topic info and the topic note feed, hot or latest
- Search keyword suggestions, image search, group-chat search
- Homepage feed and categories; Ask Dots AI answers for a question

**Trending and inspiration**
- Hot search; daily and 7-day hot-notes rankings; dark-horse viral notes
- Hot account recommendations by day, week or month
- Creator-center inspiration feeds, hot and recommended

**Products**
- Product search, product detail and related recommendations
- Review overview — average score and star distribution — and review lists

**Transcripts**
- Video-note transcription as a submit-then-fetch task

### Creator marketplace (Pugongying)

Pugongying (蒲公英) is Xiaohongshu's official brand-creator cooperation
platform, and its data is read through the same three calls:

- Creator search with audience, location, content, pricing, performance and
  cooperation filters; the filter options themselves; a separate square for
  live-commerce creators
- Creator profile with cooperation quotes, content and feature tags; similar
  creators
- Core metrics with daily trends, data summary, note performance medians and
  rates, cost-effectiveness analysis
- Follower summary, growth history over 30 or 90 days, and follower
  distribution by gender, age, region, interest and device
- Creator note lists and note cases, note detail, featured comments and the
  components mounted on a note
- Content Square leaderboards of what is hot right now; keyword overview,
  daily search index and related keywords

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="most liked Xiaohongshu notes about sunscreen this month")
find_tools(q="小红书上关于防晒霜的热门笔记")
find_tools(prefix="social/xiaohongshu")    # browse everything for Xiaohongshu
find_tools(prefix="social/pugongying")     # the creator marketplace
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work, and the platform's usual English and Chinese names all land here. Always `describe_tool` before executing —
parameters and per-call costs differ between providers.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Xiaohongshu account or Pugongying login?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh are the rankings?">
    Search, note detail and comments go to the platform at request time. The
    daily rankings describe the previous day and are refreshed once a day.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it read private notes or direct messages?">
    No. Only public notes, comments, profiles, topics and products are
    available.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
