Zhihu (知乎) is China's question-and-answer network — the place for long,
reasoned answers from practitioners, and a prime source for research on how
Chinese professionals think about a topic. It has no public API and gates most
pages behind a login. Through AgentKey your agent reads public Zhihu data the
same way it reads everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`,
`execute_tool` — with more than one provider behind the scenes, so one route
failing does not stop the task.

## What you can ask

- *"What are the top answers to this Zhihu question?"*
- *"知乎热榜现在有什么？"*
- *"Search Zhihu for discussions about AI agents from the past month."*
- *"Pull this column's recent articles and summarize them."*
- *"Who does this Zhihu user follow, and which topics do they follow?"*
- *"Get the comments on this answer, hottest first."*
- *"这个用户在知乎上写过哪些文章？"*

## What's covered

**Questions and answers**
- Question detail; the answers to a question with sorting and pagination
- Answer detail; a user's answers

**Articles and columns**
- Column article detail and the article list of a column; related articles
- A user's articles sorted by publish time or upvotes; their included (收录)
  articles
- Similar column recommendations

**Pins (想法)**
- Pin detail, pin comments, a user's pins

**Comments**
- Comments on an answer or other content, hottest or latest; replies

**Users**
- Public profile by URL token; user search
- Followers and following
- Followed topics, columns, questions and collections; created collections

**Search**
- Keyword search with result type, sort, time interval and topic display
  controls
- Dedicated search for articles, columns, topics, users, videos, ebooks, Salt
  (盐选) and scholar content
- Search suggestions and recommendations; AI search

**Trending**
- The hot list, the hot recommend feed, the video list

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="top answers to this Zhihu question")
find_tools(q="知乎热榜现在有什么")
find_tools(prefix="social/zhihu")          # browse everything for Zhihu
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work, and the platform's usual English and Chinese names all land here. Always `describe_tool` before executing — parameters and per-call
costs differ between providers.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Zhihu account?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it cover paid Salt (盐选) content?">
    Salt titles can be searched like other content types. Whether a given
    title's body is returned depends on what Zhihu serves publicly for it;
    paywalled text is not unlocked.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh is the data?">
    Every call goes to Zhihu at request time; the hot list, answers and
    comments reflect what Zhihu returns at that moment.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
