LinkedIn is the record of who works where and what professionals are saying,
and it is one of the most closed platforms on the web: the official API is
limited to approved partners and the site aggressively blocks anonymous
visitors. Through AgentKey your agent reads public LinkedIn data the same way it
reads everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — with
more than one provider behind the scenes, so one route failing does not stop
the task.

## What you can ask

- *"Summarize what this person has posted on LinkedIn in the last month."*
- *"Get the headline, location and current role from this LinkedIn profile."*
- *"Pull the comments on this post and tell me which companies the commenters work for."*
- *"What has Anthropic's company page published recently?"*
- *"Find open machine-learning engineer jobs in Berlin on LinkedIn."*
- *"Give me the full description of this job posting."*
- *"Which posts has this founder commented on or reacted to lately?"*
- *"Compare the posting cadence of these three company pages this quarter."*

## What's covered

**People**
- Profile by username or by profile URL — name, headline and the other core
  profile fields
- A member's activity, filtered by type: the posts they published, the posts
  they commented on, the posts they reacted to
- Offset and continuation-token pagination, so a long history can be walked
  page by page

**Posts**
- Detail for a post or a long-form article
- Comments sorted by relevance or by most recent, page by page, optionally
  narrowed to one share of the post

**Companies**
- Company profile — the public page's description and details
- The posts published on a company page

**Jobs**
- Job search
- Job detail for a single posting, as shown on the job page

A typical task chains these: resolve the person from a profile URL, pull their
activity, then fetch the comments on the posts that matter — or search jobs,
then open the detail of each shortlisted posting.

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="what this person has posted on LinkedIn in the last month")
find_tools(prefix="social/linkedin")       # browse everything for LinkedIn
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work, and the platform's usual English and Chinese names all land here. Always `describe_tool` before executing — parameters and
per-call costs differ between providers.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a LinkedIn account or partner API access?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it read connection-only content or send messages?">
    No. Only what a profile, company page or post exposes publicly is
    available, and every tool is read-only — no connection requests, messages
    or posting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is job data current?">
    Every call goes to LinkedIn at request time, so job search and job detail
    reflect the listings that are live at that moment.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
