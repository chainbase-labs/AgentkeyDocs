YouTube is the largest video library on the web, and the official Data API
comes with a daily quota, OAuth hurdles and no transcript endpoint — the thing
an agent most often needs. Through AgentKey your agent reads public YouTube data
the same way it reads everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`,
`execute_tool` — with several providers behind the scenes, so one route failing
does not stop the task.

## What you can ask

- *"Find the most viewed videos about 'vibe coding' uploaded this month."*
- *"Transcribe this YouTube video and give me the key points."*
- *"What do the comments on this video say — is the reaction positive?"*
- *"List the latest uploads and Shorts from the MKBHD channel."*
- *"Get this channel's description and subscriber count from its handle."*
- *"Show me videos related to this one."*
- *"What has this channel posted on its Community tab lately?"*

## What's covered

**Videos**
- Keyword search with language, upload-date, duration and sort filters; a
  separate search for Shorts
- Video detail by ID; related videos
- Stream information and a signed stream URL for playback
- Search suggestions for expanding a query

**Comments**
- Top-level comments with sorting and locale options, paginated
- Replies under a comment thread

**Transcripts and captions**
- The caption tracks available for a video, returned as SRT, XML, JSON3 or
  plain text
- Spoken-text transcript extraction, with an asynchronous job mode for videos
  that take longer to process

**Channels**
- Channel information and description; channel ID from a URL or handle, and the
  channel URL back from an ID
- A channel's videos, Shorts and Community posts
- Channel search

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="transcript of this YouTube video with key points")
find_tools(prefix="social/youtube")        # browse everything for YouTube
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work, and the platform's usual English and Chinese names all land here. Always `describe_tool` before executing — parameters and
per-call costs differ between providers.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Google API key or a YouTube quota?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does every video have a transcript?">
    Caption tracks are returned when YouTube has them, whether uploaded by the
    creator or auto-generated, and the transcript route extracts the spoken
    text. A video with no captions and no extractable speech returns nothing
    to transcribe.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it read private or unlisted videos?">
    No. Only public videos, comments and channels are available; results
    reflect what YouTube returns at request time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
