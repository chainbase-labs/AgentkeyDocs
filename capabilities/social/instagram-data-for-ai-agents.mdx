Instagram is where brands, creators and trends are made visible, and it is one
of the least agent-friendly platforms: the Graph API only covers accounts you
own, and the website demands a login after a few pages. Through
AgentKey your agent reads public Instagram data the same way it reads
everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — with several
providers behind the scenes, so one route failing does not stop the task.

## What you can ask

- *"What are the latest posts from @nike and how is each one performing?"*
- *"Pull the comments on this Reel and summarize the sentiment."*
- *"Find recent posts under #sustainablefashion."*
- *"Who follows this account, and which accounts are similar to it?"*
- *"What is being posted from Shibuya Crossing right now?"*
- *"Show me this creator's current stories and highlight reels."*
- *"Which posts use this audio track?"*
- *"Translate the top comments on this post into English."*
- *"Transcribe what is said in this Reel, with timestamps."*
- *"Does this creator publish a business email or phone number?"*

## What's covered

**Posts and Reels**
- Post detail by shortcode, media ID or URL; oEmbed info
- Likes on a post; a user's posts, Reels, reposts and tagged posts
- Reels search by keyword or hashtag; a recommended Reels feed and trending
  Reels; a web-index Reels search with a recency window
- Translation of a caption or a comment, or a batch of comments at once
- The spoken transcript of a post or Reel, with speaker labels and word-level
  timestamps
- A post's video on a hosted MP4 link that plays without an Instagram session

**Comments**
- Top-level comments with popular or newest sorting, paginated
- Replies under a comment

**Users**
- Profile by username or user ID, "about" info, former usernames
- Followers and following lists, and a name search inside either list
- Similar and related profiles; username → user ID
- The contact email and phone number a creator or business publishes on its
  profile, including the address behind the profile's Email button
- Profile search by a bio or caption keyword, optionally with each account's
  country, join month and published contact details

**Stories and highlights**
- A user's current stories, their highlight reels and the stories inside each
  highlight

**Hashtags, locations and music**
- Posts under a hashtag — Instagram's own top-ranked posts or the newest
  first, for monitoring; hashtag search
- A hashtag's total post count; a web-index hashtag search with date and
  media-type filters
- Location info, posts at a location, nearby content; locations by city or by
  coordinates, cities by country
- Music search and the posts using a given audio

**Search and explore**
- Global search; search for users, hashtags and places
- Explore page sections and the explore feed
- Shortcode ↔ media ID conversion; shortcode extraction from a URL

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="latest Instagram posts from @nike with engagement")
find_tools(prefix="social/instagram")      # browse everything for Instagram
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work, and the platform's usual English and Chinese names all land here. Always `describe_tool` before executing — parameters and
per-call costs differ between providers.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Meta developer app or a business account?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it read private accounts or direct messages?">
    No. Only public posts, Reels, stories, profiles and relationships are
    available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh is the data?">
    Every call goes to the platform at request time. Stories and highlights
    reflect what is live at that moment, so a story that has expired will not
    be returned.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
