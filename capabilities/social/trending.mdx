"What is trending right now?" is the most common question an agent gets about
social media, and the answer lives in a dozen different hot lists, each on a
platform that does not want to be read. Through AgentKey your agent reads all
of them the same way — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — and can
start from a single cross-platform ranking before drilling into one network.

## What you can ask

- *"What is trending across Chinese social platforms right now?"*
- *"今天全网热搜前十是什么？"*
- *"Has this keyword appeared on any hot list in the past two weeks, and where?"*
- *"Compare today's Weibo and Douyin hot searches — which stories are on both?"*
- *"What is trending on X in Japan and on TikTok today?"*
- *"Which Xiaohongshu notes went viral overnight?"*
- *"Which WeChat articles passed 100K reads yesterday?"*
- *"Show me the Zhihu hot list and the Bilibili popular feed."*

## What's covered

**Across platforms**
- The hot ranking of any one platform through a single uniform tool
- An aggregated cross-platform TOP10
- Keyword search across platforms' hot lists over a date range of up to 30
  days — where and when a topic surfaced

**Douyin**
- Hot search list and current hot topics; rising, city and challenge hot lists
- Video, topic, search, music and brand hot lists; hot words and trend words
- Daily hot works ranking; daily and 7-day like-surge rankings

**Weibo**
- Hot search ranking — the top ten or the full fifty; brief and categories
- Hot ranking timeline; entertainment, life and social rankings

**X (Twitter)**
- Trending topics, by country

**TikTok**
- Daily trending search words; trending hashtag list and detail
- Top-content list; music chart; daily live rank

**YouTube**
- Trending videos

**Bilibili**
- Hot search; popular feed; comprehensive popular-video information
- Huahuo creator and video rankings by category and industry

**Zhihu**
- Hot list; hot recommend feed; video list

**Kuaishou**
- Hot list with categories and board detail; hot-search person board
- Brand, live, shopping and music rankings

**Xiaohongshu**
- Hot search; daily and 7-day hot-notes rankings; dark-horse viral notes
- Hot account recommendations; creator inspiration feeds; Pugongying Content
  Square leaderboards

**WeChat**
- Original viral article ranking; 100K+ reads article ranking
- Reading-growth and overall strength rankings of Official Accounts
- WeChat Index for a keyword

**Reddit and Threads**
- Reddit popular feed and trending searches; Threads top content for a keyword

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="what is trending across Chinese social platforms right now")
find_tools(q="今天全网热搜前十")
find_tools(prefix="social/hotspot")        # the cross-platform rankings
find_tools(q="hot search ranking", prefix="social/weibo")   # scoped to one platform
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work (热搜, 热榜, trending and hot list all
route here). Always `describe_tool` before executing — some rankings are live,
some are published once a day, and the parameters differ.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How fresh is a hot list?">
    Live lists — Weibo hot search, Douyin hot search, X trends, Zhihu hot
    list, Bilibili popular — are read from the platform at request time. The
    daily rankings (Douyin hot works and like-surge, Xiaohongshu hot notes,
    WeChat viral articles) describe the previous day and are refreshed once a
    day.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I look back in time?">
    Partly. The cross-platform keyword search covers a date range of up to 30
    days, Weibo has a hot ranking timeline, and several daily rankings accept
    a date. Live lists have no history; capture them on a schedule if you need
    one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I pick a country or city?">
    X trends take a country, Douyin has a city hot list, and TikTok's search
    accepts a region. Other lists return what the platform serves for its
    default region.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
