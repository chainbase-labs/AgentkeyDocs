WeChat Channels (视频号) is the short-video feed inside WeChat, reaching the
whole WeChat user base, and it is even more closed than the rest of the app:
videos only open inside WeChat and there is no web presence to index. Through
AgentKey your agent reads public Channels data the same way it reads everything
else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — with several providers
behind the scenes, so one route failing does not stop the task.

## What you can ask

- *"Find WeChat Channels videos about camping from the past week."*
- *"这个视频号最近发了什么视频？"*
- *"From the link I copied, pull this Channels video's title, metrics and comments."*
- *"Which Channels account is bound to this Official Account?"*
- *"Transcribe this Channels video."*
- *"Is this account live right now, and what did its recent lives look like?"*
- *"Search for Channels creators about home fitness."*
- *"帮我看看这个视频号的合集里有哪些视频"*

## What's covered

**Videos**
- Resolve a copied link, preview URL or short feed ID to the video; title; full
  detail, including a realtime variant that takes the link directly
- Interaction metrics for a video; a downloadable media URL; share-URL
  generation
- Keyword search with publish-time and sort controls; search within a single
  channel
- Conversion of an encrypted export ID from search results into a usable video
  identity

**Comments**
- First-level comments, paginated; replies under a comment

**Accounts**
- Account info, profile and stats
- The account's video feed; its collections (合集) and the videos in each
- Account search by name or keyword
- The Channels account bound to an Official Account; channel ID → finder
  username

**Live**
- Live detail and live history for an account

**Transcripts**
- Video transcription as a submit-then-fetch task

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="WeChat Channels videos about camping from the past week")
find_tools(q="这个视频号最近发了什么视频")
find_tools(prefix="social/wechat_channels")   # browse everything for Channels
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work, and the platform's usual English and Chinese names all land here. Always `describe_tool` before executing —
parameters and per-call costs differ between providers, and several tools take
Channels' own identifiers (object ID, nonce ID, v2Name) that earlier calls
return.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a WeChat account?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I only have a link copied from WeChat. Is that enough?">
    Yes. A copied video link, preview URL or short feed ID resolves to the
    video's basic info and identifiers, and from there the detail, metrics and
    comment tools take over.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh is the data?">
    Every call goes to the platform at request time; the realtime detail route
    in particular reads the video as it stands right now. Nothing is served
    from a stale archive.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
