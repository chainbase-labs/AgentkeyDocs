Kuaishou (快手) is China's second short-video platform and the strongest one
outside the big cities, which makes it essential for consumer and live-commerce
research — and like Douyin it exposes no public API. Through AgentKey your agent
reads public Kuaishou data the same way it reads everything else —
`find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — with several providers behind
the scenes, so one route failing does not stop the task.

## What you can ask

- *"Find Kuaishou videos about rural cooking with the most likes."*
- *"快手热榜上现在有什么？"*
- *"Pull this Kuaishou video's details and comments."*
- *"List this creator's recent videos and their engagement."*
- *"Who is on the Kuaishou live ranking right now?"*
- *"Which brands lead the Kuaishou brand list this week?"*
- *"帮我把这条快手视频转成文字"*

## What's covered

**Videos**
- Keyword search; video detail by ID or by URL; batch lookup by IDs
- A creator's hottest works
- Comments and sub-comments, paginated

**Creators**
- Profile by user ID; user ID from a profile; account search by name
- Published videos; collections
- Live info for a creator, and their live replays

**Search**
- Comprehensive search, plus dedicated image, live, music, tag and user search
- Tag feed and the selection (recommended) feed

**Trending**
- Hot list with categories and board detail; the hot-search person board
- Brand, live, shopping and music rankings

**Transcripts and links**
- Video transcription as a submit-then-fetch task
- Short share-link resolution; share link and short-URL generation

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="Kuaishou videos about rural cooking with the most likes")
find_tools(q="快手热榜上现在有什么")
find_tools(prefix="social/kuaishou")       # browse everything for Kuaishou
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work, and the platform's usual English and Chinese names all land here. Always `describe_tool` before executing — parameters and
per-call costs differ between providers.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Kuaishou account?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does transcription work?">
    It is a two-step task: one call submits the video and returns a task ID,
    a second call fetches the finished transcript. Allow a short wait between
    the two for longer videos.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh is the data?">
    Every call goes to the platform at request time; hot lists, live rankings
    and search results reflect what Kuaishou returns at that moment.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
