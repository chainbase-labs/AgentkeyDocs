TikTok is where short-form trends start, and it is notoriously hard to read
programmatically: the official Research API is restricted to approved academics,
and the app signs every request. Through AgentKey your agent reads public TikTok
data the same way it reads everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`,
`execute_tool` — with several providers behind the scenes, so one route failing
does not stop the task.

## What you can ask

- *"Find TikTok videos about 'glass skin' posted this week in the US."*
- *"Pull this creator's latest videos with their view, like and share counts."*
- *"What are the comments under this video saying — any recurring complaints?"*
- *"Which hashtags and search terms are trending on TikTok today?"*
- *"What is on the TikTok music chart right now?"*
- *"Is this creator live, and what products are pinned in the room?"*
- *"Show me the reviews for this TikTok Shop product."*
- *"Which ads are in the Top Ads spotlight for the beauty industry?"*

## What's covered

**Videos**
- Keyword search with sort, publish-time and region controls; photo-post search
- Video detail by ID or share link, including a country-aware variant; batch
  detail; per-video metrics
- Fake-view detection; an AI-generated article summarizing a video; content
  translation

**Comments**
- Comments with pagination, and the replies under a comment
- Keyword analysis of a video's comments

**Creators**
- Profile by username or secUid; conversion between username, user ID and
  secUid
- Posts sorted latest or popular, liked videos, favorites, reposts, playlists
  and mixes, music used
- Followers and following, with search inside those lists; similar creators
- User search; creator info and milestones; an account's country

**Hashtags and music**
- Hashtag detail, hashtag search, the videos under a hashtag
- Music chart, music detail and search, the videos using a sound

**Trending**
- Daily trending search words
- Trending hashtag list and detail; top-content list and detail
- Explore and home-feed videos

**Live**
- Whether a room is live, single or in batch; room info from a link or a user
- Live search, room rankings and the daily live rank; gift lists and gift
  names
- Products pinned in a live room; live comment (danmaku) extraction

**TikTok Shop**
- Product detail and reviews; product search; products by category and by
  seller
- Product and shop IDs from a share link; videos associated with a product
- A hot-selling product list for the Southeast Asian, Japanese and Mexican
  regions

**Creator insights and ad library**
- Creator Search Insights — topics, trends and the videos behind them
- Top Ads spotlight and filters, single ad detail, keyframe and interactive
  analysis, recommended ads

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="TikTok videos about glass skin posted this week in the US")
find_tools(prefix="social/tiktok")         # browse everything for TikTok
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work, and aliases resolve (TikTok, 海外抖音 and
TT all land here; 抖音 alone routes to [Douyin](/capabilities/social/douyin-chinese-tiktok-data-for-ai-agents)).
Always `describe_tool` before executing — parameters and per-call costs differ
between providers.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a TikTok developer or Research API account?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I control which country's TikTok I see?">
    Partly. Post search accepts a region, and one video-detail route takes a
    country parameter, so results can be pinned to a market. Trending lists and
    charts return what TikTok serves for the route's default region unless the
    tool exposes a region option — check `describe_tool`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is TikTok the same as Douyin here?">
    No. They are separate platforms with separate tools; Douyin (the mainland
    China app) has [its own page](/capabilities/social/douyin-chinese-tiktok-data-for-ai-agents).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
