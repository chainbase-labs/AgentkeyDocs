Douyin (抖音) is the mainland-China sibling of TikTok and the single biggest
source of short-video attention in the country, with no public read API and
heavily signed requests. Through AgentKey your agent reads public Douyin data
the same way it reads everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`,
`execute_tool` — with more than one provider behind the scenes, so one route
failing does not stop the task.

## What you can ask

- *"Which Douyin videos about 'City Walk' got the most likes this week?"*
- *"抖音热榜现在排前十的是什么？"*
- *"Pull this Douyin video's stats, comments and a transcript."*
- *"List this creator's recent works with likes, comments and shares."*
- *"Which accounts had the biggest like surge over the last seven days?"*
- *"Is this streamer live right now, and what products are in the room?"*
- *"帮我查这个达人在星图上的报价和粉丝画像"*
- *"What are the hot challenges and hot songs on Douyin today?"*

## What's covered

**Videos (works)**
- Keyword search in several variants — by body text, by title, image-and-text,
  multi-type — plus a search for AI-generated works
- Video detail by ID, URL or share code, single or batch; like, comment, share
  and download counts
- Highest-quality play URL, danmaku (on-screen comments), related videos
- Mixes, series and collections — detail and the videos inside them
- Comments with replies, and a word cloud of a video's comments

**Creators**
- Profile by UID, sec_uid, short ID or Douyin ID; batch profiles
- Homepage videos, liked videos, collections, mixes and series
- Followers and following lists; user search; hot account recommendations
- Follower trends, fan portraits, and the topics and search terms a creator's
  fans are interested in

**Topics and hashtags**
- Hashtag detail, hashtag search and suggestions, the videos under a hashtag
- Topic search and suggestions; discussion, experience and school search
- Current hot topics and the videos related to a topic or hot spot

**Trending and rankings**
- Hot search list, total hot list, rising hot list, city hot list, hot
  challenge list
- Video, topic, search, music and brand hot lists; hot words and trend words
- Daily hot works ranking; daily and 7-day like-surge rankings
- High completion-rate, high like-rate, high follow-rate and low-follower
  breakout lists

**Live**
- Live room info by user, sec_uid or room ID; live search and rooms by nickname
- Live hot search list, gift-sender rankings, products in a live room, live
  history
- Danmaku extraction from a live room

**Creator, brand and campaign analytics**
- Xingtu (星图) KOL data — base info, Xingtu index, fan portrait, conversion
  ability, service price, video performance; KOL search and commercial
  rankings
- Juliang Suanshu (巨量算数) trends — content publish, consumption and
  interaction trends, keyword heat and interpretation, audience portraits,
  brand index, trend lines and radar
- Creator-center billboards: hot topics, challenges, music, props and the
  material center
- DOU+ cost estimation and video rankings; author diagnosis; per-video analysis
  and reports

**Transcripts and utilities**
- Video transcription as a submit-then-fetch task
- Share short-link and QR generation; video, user and live-room ID extraction
  from links

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="Douyin videos about City Walk with the most likes this week")
find_tools(q="抖音热榜现在排前十的是什么")
find_tools(prefix="social/douyin")         # browse everything for Douyin
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work, and aliases resolve (抖音, Douyin and
"Chinese TikTok" all land here; TikTok proper has
[its own page](/capabilities/social/tiktok)). Always `describe_tool` before
executing — parameters and per-call costs differ between providers.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Douyin account or a Xingtu login?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh are the rankings?">
    Search, video detail, comments and live status go to the platform at
    request time. The daily rankings describe the previous day and are
    refreshed once a day.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Douyin the same as TikTok here?">
    No. They are separate platforms with separate tools and separate Scenario
    Methods; TikTok has [its own page](/capabilities/social/tiktok).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
