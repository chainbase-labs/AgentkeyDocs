Founders and executives are the thread that connects companies: who started
what, who moved where, who backed whom. Crunchbase keeps that professional
record, and through AgentKey your agent reads it the same way it reads
everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — resolving a
name to a person identifier and then pulling the profile and its related
cards.

## What you can ask

- *"Who is the CEO of this company, and where did they work before?"*
- *"Which companies has this person founded or invested in?"*
- *"Which key executives joined or left this company recently?"*
- *"Where did this founder study?"*
- *"Which companies announced layoffs this quarter?"*
- *"Is this company involved in any legal proceedings?"*
- *"Which conferences has this founder spoken at?"*
- *"这位创始人的职业履历是什么？"*

## What's covered

**People**
- Person lookup by identifier, with cards for degrees, event appearances,
  founded organizations, jobs, the primary job, participated funding rounds,
  funds and investments, partner funding rounds and investments, and press
  references
- Search people with complex filters expressed in the request body;
  autocomplete a name to a person identifier
- Principals — people and organizations under one entity type — lookup and
  search

**Jobs and roles**
- Job lookup, with cards for the organization and the person; search jobs
- Key employee changes — lookup and search
- Degrees, with cards for the school organization and the person; search
  degrees

**Workforce and legal events**
- Layoffs — lookup and search; layoff predictions
- Legal proceedings — lookup and search
- Event appearances — lookup and search, for speakers and participants at
  events

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="Crunchbase profile of a founder with past jobs and education")
find_tools(q="这家公司最近的高管变动")
find_tools(prefix="business/crunchbase")  # browse every Crunchbase entity type
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work. Always `describe_tool` before
executing — card lookups take a card name, search tools take a structured
query body, and the per-call cost is shown there. Resolve the person
identifier with autocomplete first.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** The same capability is available as a fixed-contract
  [Scenario Method](/api-reference/scenario/overview) with automatic provider
  failover:
  [`business.person.get`](/api-reference/scenario/methods/business-person-get)
  returns one normalized person profile by provider id.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What personal data does this expose?">
    Only Crunchbase's professional record: roles, education, founded
    companies, investments, press mentions and public event appearances. It
    is a business dataset about public professional activity, read at request
    time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it cover people in crypto?">
    Crunchbase covers founders and executives across industries. For crypto
    KOLs, founders and investors ranked by X influence, see
    [Crypto projects and VCs](/capabilities/business/crypto-projects-and-vc-data-for-ai-agents),
    backed by RootData.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is historical data available?">
    Yes. Past jobs and degrees sit on the person record alongside the current
    role, and key-employee changes, layoffs and legal proceedings are
    searchable records rather than a live snapshot.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
