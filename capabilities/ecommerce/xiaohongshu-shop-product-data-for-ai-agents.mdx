Xiaohongshu (RedNote) is where Chinese consumers discover products through
other people's notes, and its shop sits right next to that discovery. The
catalog is app-first and has no public API. Through AgentKey your agent
searches Xiaohongshu Shop the same way it reads everything else —
`find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — and can carry the results
into a deeper look on another marketplace.

## What you can ask

- *"小红书商城搜 '防晒霜'，列出前 20 个商品和价格。"*
- *"Search Xiaohongshu Shop for 'linen dress' and keep paging until you have 60 results."*
- *"Compare the first page of Xiaohongshu Shop results for 'ceramic mug' with the same search on Taobao."*

## What's covered

**Search**
- Keyword search of Xiaohongshu Shop products with page and search-ID
  pagination — the first page returns a search ID that later pages require

Not covered in the catalog today: product detail by ID, reviews and shop
pages for Xiaohongshu Shop. Product detail and reviews are available as
Scenario Methods (see below). For a product you find here, open the matching
listing on [Taobao / Tmall](/capabilities/ecommerce/taobao-and-tmall-product-data-for-ai-agents) or
[JD.com](/capabilities/ecommerce/jd-product-data-for-ai-agents) to read details and reviews through the
catalog.

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="小红书商城搜防晒霜")
find_tools(q="search Xiaohongshu Shop for linen dresses")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/xiaohongshu-ec")  # browse everything for Xiaohongshu Shop
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Prices come back in yuan, as the listing shows them, not converted. Aliases
resolve (小红书, Xiaohongshu and RedNote all land here). Always
`describe_tool` before executing: it shows how to pass the search ID for pages
after the first, and the per-call cost.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** The same capability is available as fixed-contract
  [Scenario Methods](/api-reference/scenario/overview) with automatic provider
  failover, and the method set is broader than the catalog above:
  [`ecommerce.xiaohongshu_shop.product.search`](/api-reference/scenario/methods/ecommerce-xiaohongshu_shop-product-search),
  [`ecommerce.xiaohongshu_shop.product.get`](/api-reference/scenario/methods/ecommerce-xiaohongshu_shop-product-get),
  [`ecommerce.xiaohongshu_shop.product.reviews`](/api-reference/scenario/methods/ecommerce-xiaohongshu_shop-product-reviews).
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Xiaohongshu account?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I open a product or read its reviews?">
    Not through the catalog tools today, which cover search. The Scenario
    Methods listed above add product detail and reviews behind a fixed
    contract.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh are the results?">
    Every call goes to the platform at request time; nothing is served from a
    stale archive. Results are what Xiaohongshu Shop returns for that keyword
    at that moment, in yuan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
