Xianyu (闲鱼, also branded Goofish) is China's largest second-hand
marketplace, and it is app-first: there is no public API and the web view
shows little without a login. Through AgentKey your agent reads public Xianyu
listings the same way it reads everything else — `find_tools`,
`describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — which makes it a quick source of real
resale prices and supply for a product you are researching elsewhere.

## What you can ask

- *"闲鱼上搜 'iPad Air 5'，按价格从低到高，列出前 15 个。"*
- *"Find Sony A7 III bodies listed on Xianyu recently, newest first."*
- *"Which Xianyu listings for this lens come from the sellers with the best credit?"*
- *"Open this Xianyu listing and tell me the asking price and what the seller wrote."*
- *"What are used Switch OLED consoles going for on Xianyu right now?"*

## What's covered

**Search**
- Keyword search of second-hand listings with page controls
- Sort by activity, recency, seller credit, price or listing time

**Listing details**
- The public detail of a listing by item ID — inspect a known resale item
  after search or link discovery

Not covered today: seller profiles, messaging, bidding and anything behind a
buyer login. For the new-item price to compare against, search the same
product on [Taobao / Tmall](/capabilities/ecommerce/taobao-tmall) or
[JD.com](/capabilities/ecommerce/jd).

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="闲鱼上 iPad Air 5 按价格从低到高")
find_tools(q="recently listed used camera bodies on Xianyu")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/xianyu")       # browse everything for Xianyu
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Prices come back in yuan, as the listing shows them, not converted. Aliases
resolve (闲鱼, Xianyu and Goofish all land here). Always `describe_tool` before
executing: it shows the sort options, the item ID the detail lookup expects,
and the per-call cost.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Xianyu account?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the agent message a seller or make an offer?">
    No. Access is read-only: search and listing detail. Negotiation happens in
    the app, by a person.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh are the listings?">
    Every call goes to Xianyu at request time; nothing is served from a stale
    archive. Second-hand listings disappear once sold, so a detail lookup on
    an older ID may return nothing — search again for current supply.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
