Amazon is the reference catalog for most product research, and one of the
harder ones for an agent to read: the official Product Advertising API needs an
approved associate account that keeps selling, and the storefront blocks
automated browsing. Through AgentKey your agent reads public Amazon listings the
same way it reads everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`,
`execute_tool` — choosing the country marketplace on every call.

## What you can ask

- *"Find wireless earbuds under 50 dollars on Amazon US, Prime-eligible only, sorted by rating."*
- *"Pull the listing for this ASIN on Amazon Germany and give me the price and rating."*
- *"What do the top reviews of this product say? Summarize the recurring complaints."*
- *"Show me the current Best Sellers in Home & Kitchen on Amazon UK."*
- *"List the products under this category node on Amazon Japan, newest first."*
- *"Is the same ASIN cheaper on amazon.com or amazon.co.uk right now?"*
- *"Are there any deals on standing desks on Amazon today?"*
- *"Search Amazon for used copies of this camera body."*
- *"Who else sells this ASIN, and who holds the Buy Box?"*
- *"What is this seller's feedback rating, and what else does their storefront carry?"*

## What's covered

**Search**
- Keyword or ASIN search inside a chosen country marketplace
- Sort order, item condition, Prime-only and deals filters, page controls
- Search straight from an Amazon search or category page URL

**Product details**
- Listing details for a known ASIN in a selected marketplace — for catalog
  review, side-by-side comparison or downstream analysis
- Full details from a product URL — variants, attributes, images, ratings and
  review count — and a batch of ASINs looked up in one call

**Reviews**
- The top reviews of a product by ASIN — the prominent customer feedback,
  ready for sentiment or quality analysis
- A larger page of reviews by ASIN or URL — rating, title, text, date and
  verified-purchase badge — filtered by star rating, keyword, date range and
  verified purchases, sorted most helpful or most recent

**Offers and sellers**
- Every offer on a product — the Buy Box first, then the other sellers — with
  price, condition, seller name and ID, seller rating, Prime, delivery
  estimate and fulfillment
- A seller's profile by seller ID — star rating, positive-feedback share,
  feedback counts for 30 days, 90 days, 12 months and lifetime, and the
  registered business name and address
- A seller's storefront, page by page — ASIN, title, price, list price,
  rating, review count, and coupon, sponsored, Prime, best-seller and Amazon's
  Choice flags

**Rankings & categories**
- Best Sellers for a category path, paginated, in the marketplace you choose
- Products under a numeric category node with configurable sorting — browse
  an assortment or collect a whole category's listings
- Today's deals for a marketplace — deal price, list price, discount, deal
  type and badge

**Marketplaces**
- Operations take a country or marketplace (or read it from the URL you
  pass), so the same ASIN, keyword or category can be checked across Amazon's
  regional sites one call at a time

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="best sellers in home and kitchen on Amazon UK")
find_tools(q="亚马逊上搜降噪耳机，只看 Prime 商品")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/amazon")       # browse everything for Amazon
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Prices come back in the currency of the marketplace you selected, not
converted. Always `describe_tool` before executing: it shows the country field
and its accepted values, the sort and filter options, and the per-call cost.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an Amazon seller, associate or developer account?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which Amazon marketplaces can I query?">
    Most operations take a country or marketplace parameter that selects the
    regional site; tools that take a product, search or category URL read it
    from the URL. Run `describe_tool` on the operation to see the accepted
    values rather than assuming a country is supported.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh are prices, rankings and reviews?">
    Every call goes to Amazon at request time; nothing is served from a stale
    archive. A price is what the listing shows at that moment, in the
    marketplace's own currency. AgentKey does not keep a price history — to
    track a price, call again on your own schedule.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
