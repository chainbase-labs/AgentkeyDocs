Shopee is the marketplace to watch in Southeast Asia and Taiwan, and it is
awkward to reach: each country runs as its own site, the official Open Platform
only serves sellers managing their own shops, and the storefront is a
JavaScript app that resists scraping. Through AgentKey your agent reads public
Shopee data the same way it reads everything else — `find_tools`,
`describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — picking the site on each call. Shopee has
the deepest coverage in this category, from search facets down to the review
split per SKU.

## What you can ask

- *"Search Shopee Taiwan for 行動電源 and list the ten cheapest with their ratings."*
- *"Which categories does Shopee Indonesia return for the keyword 'air fryer'?"*
- *"Open this Shopee item and give me every SKU option with its price."*
- *"How do the ratings on this item split across its variants, and what are the review tags?"*
- *"Pull only the one- and two-star reviews for this listing."*
- *"What installment plans does Shopee Taiwan offer on this item?"*
- *"Profile this Shopee shop: rating summary, categories, and what it is listing right now."*
- *"List everything this shop sells on Shopee Thailand and flag anything under 500 baht."*

## What's covered

**Search**
- Keyword search inside a marketplace site
- Category facets for a keyword — how the results split by category before
  you drill in

**Product details & SKUs**
- Item details and a display snapshot by site and item ID
- The SKU option matrix, and the observed SKU models with offset and rating
  filters
- Installment plans and amounts (Taiwan and Indonesia; not Thailand)

**Reviews**
- Item reviews with offset pagination and a rating filter
- Review tags, and how reviews distribute across the item's SKU models

**Shops**
- Basic profile and detailed shop information by shop username; profile and
  rating summary by shop ID
- Shop categories and the shop's item cards
- Shop reviews, plain or enriched, with pagination and rating filters

**Sites**
- Every operation targets one marketplace site; the catalog descriptions
  reference Taiwan, Indonesia and Thailand

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="cheapest power banks on Shopee Taiwan with ratings")
find_tools(q="蝦皮台灣這個商品的評價和規格")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/shopee")       # browse everything for Shopee
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Prices come back in the site's own currency, not converted. Always
`describe_tool` before executing: it shows the site field and its accepted
values, the identifier each operation expects (item ID, shop username or shop
ID), and the per-call cost.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Shopee seller or Open Platform account?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which Shopee sites are available?">
    Every operation takes a site parameter. The catalog descriptions reference
    Taiwan, Indonesia and Thailand, and the installment lookup is explicitly
    limited to Taiwan and Indonesia. Check `describe_tool` for the accepted
    values of the operation you are about to call.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh are prices, stock and reviews?">
    Every call goes to Shopee at request time; nothing is served from a stale
    archive. Prices are what the site shows at that moment, in its own currency.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
