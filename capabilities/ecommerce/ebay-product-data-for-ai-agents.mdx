eBay is where used, refurbished, collectible and hard-to-find goods change
hands, and its sold listings are the closest thing to a market price for
second-hand goods. eBay's own APIs need a registered developer application,
and completed-sale data is restricted to approved partners. Through AgentKey
your agent reads public eBay listings the same way it reads everything else —
`find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — including what items actually
sold for.

## What you can ask

- *"Search eBay for a used Nintendo Switch OLED under 250 dollars, Buy It Now only, cheapest first."*
- *"Which auctions for vintage Levi's 501 end soonest?"*
- *"Open this eBay listing — price, shipping, condition, seller feedback, and how many people are watching."*
- *"How many bids does this auction have, and when does it end?"*
- *"What did used iPhone 13 Pro phones actually sell for on eBay last month?"*
- *"Give me price comps for this camera lens from recent sales, new versus used."*
- *"Did sellers accept Best Offers on recent sales of this model?"*
- *"eBay 上二手 Switch 最近的成交价是多少？"*

## What's covered

**Search**
- Active listings by keyword, filtered by price range, item condition (new,
  open box, refurbished, used, for parts) and listing format (auction or Buy
  It Now)
- Sort by best match, ending soonest, newly listed or price
- Title, price, condition, shipping and seller for each result

**Listing details**
- One listing by URL — price, condition, shipping, item location, seller
  feedback, quantity and watchers
- Live auction state: bid count and end time
- The eBay site comes from the listing URL, so an ebay.co.uk or ebay.de link
  returns that site's listing in its currency

**Sold listings**
- Recently sold listings for a keyword — sold price, sale date, condition,
  seller and item details — filtered by price range, condition, category,
  sold-date window and listing format, sorted by most recently ended or price,
  page by page
- A lighter variant built for quick price comps: sold price, sale date,
  condition, format, bids and whether a Best Offer was accepted, without
  seller details
- Sold-listing coverage is the US site (ebay.com) today

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="what used iPhone 13 Pro phones sold for on eBay last month")
find_tools(q="eBay 上二手 Switch 的成交价")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/ebay")         # browse everything for eBay
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Search and sold-listing results carry each item's id and URL, so search →
listing detail needs no manual parsing. Prices come back as eBay shows them,
not converted. Always `describe_tool` before executing: it shows the accepted
condition, format and sort values, the date format for the sold-date window,
and the per-call cost.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an eBay developer or seller account?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it bid, buy or message a seller?">
    No. Every tool is read-only — search, listing details and sold listings. No
    bid, purchase, offer or message is ever sent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh are prices and auction states?">
    Every call goes to eBay at request time; nothing is served from a stale
    archive. Auction bid counts and prices move until the listing ends, so
    re-check a live auction before acting on it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
