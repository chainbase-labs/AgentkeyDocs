JD.com is China's largest direct retailer and the benchmark for electronics
and appliance pricing there. Its open platform serves merchants only, and the
storefront asks for a login almost immediately. Through AgentKey your agent
reads public JD.com data the same way it reads everything else — `find_tools`,
`describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — including a lightweight price check for
when the full listing is more than you need.

## What you can ask

- *"京东搜 'iPhone 16 128G'，列出前 10 个结果和价格。"*
- *"这个京东商品现在多少钱？"*
- *"Pull the details of this JD item and summarize the listing."*
- *"Summarize the buyer comments on this JD product — what do people complain about most?"*
- *"List the products in this JD shop and collect their item IDs."*
- *"Check the current JD price of these five items and tell me which ones changed since my last check."*

## What's covered

**Search**
- Keyword search with page-based pagination — discover products and collect
  item IDs for follow-up lookups

**Product details & price**
- Details for a known item ID — inspect an individual product after finding
  its identifier
- A separate current-price lookup by item ID, for a quick check before
  comparison or purchase analysis
- The description images and product attributes for an item ID — the
  listing's long-form introduction

**Comments**
- Paginated customer comments for a product by item ID — buyer feedback for a
  known product

**Shops**
- A page of products from a shop by shop ID — browse a seller's catalog and
  collect item IDs

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="京东上这个商品现在的价格")
find_tools(q="buyer comments on this JD.com product")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/jd")           # browse everything for JD.com
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Prices come back in yuan, as JD shows them, not converted. Aliases resolve
(京东, JD and JD.com all land here). Always `describe_tool` before executing:
several operations are listed in more than one version, and it shows the exact
parameters, the identifier expected (item ID or shop ID) and the per-call cost.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a JD.com account or merchant access?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a way to check only the price?">
    Yes. A dedicated price lookup returns the current price for an item ID
    without the rest of the listing. `describe_tool` shows what it costs
    compared with the full detail lookup.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh are prices and comments?">
    Every call goes to JD.com at request time; nothing is served from a stale
    archive. A price is what the product shows at that moment, in yuan.
    AgentKey does not keep a price history — to track a price, call again on
    your own schedule.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
