Capterra is where small and mid-sized businesses pick software, and its
reviews are built for exactly that decision: an overall score with
sub-ratings, a pros and cons pair, and the reviewer's job title, company size
and industry, so a buyer can weigh reviews from companies like their own.
Through AgentKey your agent reads Capterra reviews for any product the same
way it reads everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool`
— sorted the way the question needs.

## What you can ask

- *"Compare Capterra pros and cons for Slack by reviewer company size."*
- *"What do Capterra reviewers in healthcare say about this practice-management tool?"*
- *"Pull the most complete Capterra reviews of this product and summarize the cons."*
- *"How does this CRM score on Capterra's sub-ratings, and where does it fall short?"*
- *"Show me the lowest-rated Capterra reviews of this product from companies with fewer than 50 employees."*
- *"Open this Capterra product URL and give me the newest reviews with each reviewer's job title."*
- *"Which pros come up most often in the highest-rated Capterra reviews of this tool?"*
- *"Capterra 上中小企业对这款 CRM 的评价怎么样？"*

## What's covered

**Identifiers**
- Reviews for a product by the URL of its Capterra product page

**Ordering and limit**
- Most recent by default, or the most complete reviews first, highest rated
  or lowest rated
- A `limit` on how many reviews come back; set it to fetch only as many as
  you need

**What comes back**
- Overall rating and Capterra's sub-ratings for each review
- Pros and cons as the reviewer wrote them
- The reviewer's job title, company size and industry

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="Capterra pros and cons for Slack by reviewer company size")
find_tools(q="Capterra 上中小企业对这款 CRM 的评价")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/capterra")     # browse everything for Capterra
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Capterra sits in the E-commerce branch of the catalog. The review tool takes
the product's Capterra URL, so a link copied from Capterra works as is. Set a
`limit` to fetch only as many reviews as you need. Always `describe_tool`
before executing: it shows the accepted sort values and the per-call cost.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** Capterra is available through the catalog tools
  above only; there are no fixed-contract Scenario Methods for it yet. See the
  [Scenario Methods overview](/api-reference/scenario/overview) for the
  platforms that have them.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Capterra vendor account?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it reply to, flag or post reviews?">
    No. The tool is read-only — reviews come out, nothing goes in. Nothing is
    posted, answered or reported.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What isn't covered?">
    Product search and product profiles: there is no Capterra search tool, so
    start from the product's Capterra URL. Reviews of the same product on
    [G2](/capabilities/ecommerce/g2-reviews-for-ai-agents) are read by its G2
    slug or URL, and consumer brands are reviewed on
    [Trustpilot](/capabilities/ecommerce/trustpilot-reviews-for-ai-agents).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
