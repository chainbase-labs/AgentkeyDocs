Taobao and Tmall together are China's largest marketplace, and for an agent
outside Alibaba's ecosystem they are nearly sealed: the open platform wants a
registered Chinese business and app approval, and the storefront demands a
login before it shows a search result. Through AgentKey your agent reads public
Taobao and Tmall data the same way it reads everything else — `find_tools`,
`describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — across both storefronts in one place.

## What you can ask

- *"淘宝搜 '无线降噪耳机'，价格 200 到 500，只看天猫，按销量排序。"*
- *"这个淘宝商品的评价怎么样？差评主要集中在哪些问题？"*
- *"Pull the details for this Tmall item and tell me the price and the seller."*
- *"What are buyers asking about this product, and what does the discussion say?"*
- *"List everything this Taobao seller is selling, sorted by sales."*
- *"Find the cheapest Tmall listings for this phone model and compare them side by side."*

## What's covered

**Search**
- Keyword search across Taobao and Tmall with page and sort controls
- A Tmall-only switch and an inclusive price range

**Product details**
- Details for a Taobao or Tmall item by item ID — several versions of the
  lookup are listed and act as siblings, so one unhealthy route is not the end

**Reviews & Q&A**
- Product reviews by item ID, paginated and sortable
- The product's social feed — buyer questions and related discussion — for
  customer-concern research

**Shops**
- A seller's products by seller or user ID, paginated, in default or sales
  order

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="淘宝上 200 到 500 元的无线降噪耳机，只看天猫")
find_tools(q="reviews and buyer questions for this Tmall product")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/taobao")       # browse everything for Taobao and Tmall
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Prices come back in yuan, as the listing shows them, not converted. Aliases
resolve (淘宝, 天猫, Taobao and Tmall all land here). Always `describe_tool`
before executing: it shows which identifier each operation expects (item ID,
seller ID or user ID), the sort and filter options, and the per-call cost.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Taobao account or Alibaba open-platform access?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does this cover both Taobao and Tmall?">
    Yes. Search spans both and has a Tmall-only switch; details, reviews,
    buyer questions and shop listings work with an item, seller or user ID
    from either storefront.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh are prices and reviews?">
    Every call goes to the platform at request time; nothing is served from a
    stale archive. A price is what the listing shows at that moment, in yuan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
