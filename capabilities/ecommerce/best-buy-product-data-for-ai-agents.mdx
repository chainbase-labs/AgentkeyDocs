Best Buy is the US reference for consumer electronics, and its product pages
carry the two identifiers that matter most for matching a TV, laptop or
appliance across retailers: the manufacturer's model number and the UPC. The
storefront blocks automated browsing, and Best Buy's developer API is limited
to approved partners. Through AgentKey your agent reads public Best Buy
listings the same way it reads everything else — `find_tools`,
`describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — search, product pages and customer reviews.

## What you can ask

- *"Search Best Buy for a 65-inch OLED TV under 1,500 dollars, highest rated first."*
- *"Which 4K monitors on Best Buy are newest? Give me title, brand and price."*
- *"Get the model number, UPC and description of this Best Buy TV."*
- *"Open this Best Buy laptop page and pull the price, brand, rating and images."*
- *"What are the most helpful reviews for this Best Buy soundbar? Summarize the complaints."*
- *"Show me the oldest reviews of this washer — did early buyers hit the same problem?"*
- *"Take this Best Buy TV's model number and check the price at Walmart and Target."*
- *"Best Buy 上这款显示器的差评主要说什么？"*

## What's covered

**Search**
- Keyword search with page controls and a price range
- Sort by best match, best sellers, price, rating or newest
- Title, price, rating and brand for each result

**Product details**
- One product by its page URL — title, price, brand, model number, UPC,
  rating, description and images
- The URL must carry the product's SKU, which the URLs search returns do

**Reviews**
- Customer reviews for a product by URL, a page of ten at a time — rating,
  title, text, date, reviewer and verified-purchase badge
- Newest first (the default), oldest first, by relevance, most helpful, or
  highest or lowest rating

**Store and currency**
- Coverage is bestbuy.com, Best Buy's US site, priced in US dollars

The model number and UPC are the handle for cross-retailer matching: search
[Walmart](/capabilities/ecommerce/walmart-product-data-for-ai-agents) and
[Target](/capabilities/ecommerce/target-product-data-for-ai-agents) for the
model number to find the same item there,
[Amazon](/capabilities/ecommerce/amazon-product-data-for-ai-agents) for its
marketplace price, and
[eBay](/capabilities/ecommerce/ebay-product-data-for-ai-agents) sold listings
for what a used unit resells for.

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="model number and UPC of this Best Buy TV")
find_tools(q="Best Buy 上这款显示器的差评")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/best-buy")     # browse everything for Best Buy
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Product and review tools take the product page URL, and search results carry
it — use the URL a search returns, since Best Buy's must carry the SKU. Prices
come back as Best Buy shows them, in US dollars, not converted. Always
`describe_tool` before executing: it shows the accepted sort values, the URL
form product details expects, the page limits, and the per-call cost.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** Best Buy is available through the catalog tools

  [Scenario Methods overview](/api-reference/scenario/overview) for the
  platforms that have them.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Best Buy account or developer key?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it buy, reserve for pickup, or check a store's shelf?">
    No. Every tool is read-only — search, product details and reviews — and
    what comes back is what bestbuy.com shows online, not the stock of a
    particular store near you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does product details need a specific URL?">
    Best Buy identifies a product by its SKU, and the product-details tool
    reads the SKU from the URL. The URL a search returns carries it; if you
    paste a link from elsewhere, keep the whole URL rather than trimming it,
    and if a lookup still fails, search for the product and use the URL that
    comes back.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
