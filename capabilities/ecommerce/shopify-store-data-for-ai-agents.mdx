A large share of direct-to-consumer brands sell on Shopify, each from its own
domain, so there is no single marketplace to search: every store is its own
catalog. Through AgentKey your agent reads any public Shopify store by its
domain — `allbirds.com`, `kith.com` — the same way it reads everything else —
`find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — without the store owner's
Admin API access.

## What you can ask

- *"Is this brand's website a Shopify store? What currency and country does it sell from, and where does it ship?"*
- *"List every product this store published in the last 30 days, with prices."*
- *"Which products on kith.com are on sale right now and still in stock?"*
- *"Show every variant of this product with its SKU, price and stock."*
- *"Search this store for 'wool runners' and compare the matches."*
- *"How many products and collections does this store publish, and which social accounts does it link to?"*
- *"Filter this store's catalog to one vendor and product type."*
- *"这个 Shopify 店铺最近上新了哪些商品？"*

## What's covered

**Store profile**
- Name, description, currency, country and city, the countries it ships to,
  published product and collection counts, and social links
- A domain that is not a public Shopify store comes back as not found — a
  quick check of whether a site runs on Shopify

**Product catalog**
- A store's products, page by page — title, vendor, type, tags, price range,
  sale price, stock, images, options, and every variant's SKU, price and stock
- Filters for vendor, product type, tag, title, SKU, price range, on sale, in
  stock and recently published

**Product details**
- One product by its handle or product page URL, with the full variant list

**Store search**
- The store's own search for a keyword, returning its top matches with title,
  vendor, type, tags, price range, sale price, stock and images

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="products this Shopify store published in the last 30 days")
find_tools(q="这个 Shopify 店铺最近上新了哪些商品")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/shopify")      # browse everything for Shopify
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Address a store by its domain or URL. Prices come back in the store's own
currency, not converted. Catalog filters are applied to each page the store
returns, so a filtered page can be short — or empty — while more pages remain;
keep paging until the response says there are no more. Always `describe_tool`
before executing: it shows every filter and the per-call cost.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** Shopify stores are available through the catalog
  tools above only; there are no fixed-contract Scenario Methods for them yet.
  See the [Scenario Methods overview](/api-reference/scenario/overview) for the
  platforms that have them.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need the store's permission or a Shopify API key?">
    No. The tools read what a store's public storefront publishes, and AgentKey
    holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it see orders, revenue or customers?">
    No. Only the public storefront is covered — the store profile, products,
    variants and stock status. Every tool is read-only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which stores work?">
    Any public Shopify store, addressed by its domain or URL — a store on its
    own domain is addressed by that domain. A domain that is not a public
    Shopify store comes back as not found.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
