Etsy is the marketplace for handmade, vintage and print-on-demand goods, and
much of what sells there is defined by the shop behind it — its sales, its
reviews, its range. Etsy's Open API needs a registered app with an approved
key. Through AgentKey your agent reads public Etsy listings and shops the same
way it reads everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`,
`execute_tool`.

## What you can ask

- *"Search Etsy for personalized dog collars under 30 dollars, newest first."*
- *"What are the top Etsy results for 'habit tracker 2027', and which of them are ads?"*
- *"Open this Etsy listing: price, variations, materials and where it ships from."*
- *"How many sales and reviews does this Etsy shop have, and what is its rating?"*
- *"List everything this shop currently sells, with prices."*
- *"Which listings in these results are discounted from their original price?"*
- *"Etsy 上搜手工银饰，按价格从低到高。"*

## What's covered

**Search**
- Keyword search — the same results an Etsy search or /market page shows —
  sorted by relevance, newest or price, with a price range and page controls
- Title, price, original price, image, shop, rating and an ad flag for each
  listing

**Listings**
- One listing by id: price, images, description, rating and review count,
  shop, category, variations, stock, ship-from country, favorites and
  materials

**Shops**
- A shop by name: location, sales, active listings, rating, review count,
  followers, description, logo and banner
- A shop's active listings, a page at a time, with title, price, original
  price and image

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="personalized dog collars on Etsy under 30 dollars")
find_tools(q="Etsy 上搜手工银饰")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/etsy")         # browse everything for Etsy
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

The listing id is the number in a listing URL, and search results carry both
the id and the shop name, so search → listing → shop chains without manual
parsing. Always `describe_tool` before executing: it shows the sort values,
the currency of the price filters, and the per-call cost.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** Etsy is available through the catalog tools above
  only; there are no fixed-contract Scenario Methods for it yet. See the
  [Scenario Methods overview](/api-reference/scenario/overview) for the
  platforms that have them.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an Etsy developer or seller account?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it read a shop's orders or revenue?">
    No. Only what Etsy shows publicly — listings, the shop page with its sales
    count and reviews summary. Every tool is read-only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh are prices and stock?">
    Every call goes to Etsy at request time; nothing is served from a stale
    archive. A price is what the listing shows at that moment.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
