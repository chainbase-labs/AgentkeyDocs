IKEA is the flat-pack furniture reference, and its catalogue is the kind an
agent searches rather than browses: a product family name or a plain
description — bookshelf, standing desk, bedside table — turns into a list of
items with a price and a rating on each. ikea.com has no public product API
and blocks automated browsing. Through AgentKey your agent searches IKEA's US
store the same way it reads everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`,
`execute_tool`. Search is the only IKEA tool today.

## What you can ask

- *"Find bookshelves at IKEA US with their prices and ratings."*
- *"Search IKEA for standing desks and tell me the cheapest one that comes back."*
- *"Which KALLAX units show up on IKEA US, and how are they rated?"*
- *"Show me the first two pages of IKEA results for 'bedside table', with images."*
- *"Pull IKEA's price for a BILLY bookcase so I can compare it with this Target bookshelf."*
- *"宜家美国站的书架有哪些，价格多少？"*

## What's covered

**Search**
- Keyword search of IKEA's US store, page by page
- Title, price, rating and image for each result
- No sort order or price filter today — results come back in IKEA's own
  order, so sort and filter them on the agent side

**Store and currency**
- Coverage is ikea.com's US store, priced in US dollars

Not covered today: product details by URL — description, dimensions, variants
or stock — and customer reviews; the rating on each search result is the only
review signal. For a furniture price check that needs a product page, pair
this page with
[Target](/capabilities/ecommerce/target-product-data-for-ai-agents) or
[Walmart](/capabilities/ecommerce/walmart-product-data-for-ai-agents), and
see [product research workflows](/capabilities/ecommerce/product-research-workflows-for-ai-agents)
for how the retailer tools chain together.

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="bookshelves at IKEA US with prices and ratings")
find_tools(q="宜家美国站的书架价格")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/ikea")         # browse everything for IKEA
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Search takes a keyword and a page number, nothing else. Prices come back as
IKEA shows them, in US dollars, not converted. Always `describe_tool` before
executing: it shows the page limits and the per-call cost.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** IKEA is available through the catalog tools above

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an IKEA account or IKEA Family membership?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it check my local store's stock or place an order?">
    No. The tool is read-only, and search results carry no stock at all —
    title, price, rating and image are what ikea.com's US store shows online.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it open an IKEA product page or read reviews?">
    Not today. Search is the only IKEA tool: it returns a title, price, rating
    and image per result, and the rating is the only review signal. A
    product's description, dimensions, variants, stock and review text are not
    available through AgentKey yet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
