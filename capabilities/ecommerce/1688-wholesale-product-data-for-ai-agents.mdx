1688 is Alibaba's domestic wholesale marketplace, where factories and
distributors list the offers behind much of what sells at retail elsewhere.
Sourcing teams live on it, but it is Chinese-language, login-walled and
hostile to automation. Through AgentKey your agent reads public 1688 listings
the same way it reads everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`,
`execute_tool` — and can hand a retail product straight to a wholesale
search. For suppliers that sell abroad, the same calls also search
Alibaba.com, the group's international wholesale site.

## What you can ask

- *"1688 上搜 '不锈钢保温杯'，列出前 20 个货源和价格。"*
- *"Search 1688 for 'silicone phone case' and shortlist suppliers worth contacting."*
- *"Open this 1688 listing and summarize the offer."*
- *"Find a 1688 wholesale source for this product I found on Amazon."*
- *"Search Alibaba.com for bluetooth speaker suppliers and compare their minimum order quantities."*

## What's covered

**Search**
- Keyword search of wholesale listings — discover candidate products or
  suppliers during sourcing and market research

**Listing details**
- The public detail of a wholesale listing by item ID — review a known offer
  during sourcing or catalog research

**Alibaba.com (international)**
- Keyword search of Alibaba.com wholesale listings — title, price range,
  minimum order and supplier for each
- Alibaba.com is a different site from 1688: the group's international,
  English-language B2B marketplace, with its own suppliers, listings and
  prices, aimed at buyers outside China

Not covered today: supplier company profiles, reviews, order placement and
anything behind a buyer login on either site, and listing details on
Alibaba.com. To check how a candidate product sells at
retail, pair this page with [Taobao / Tmall](/capabilities/ecommerce/taobao-and-tmall-product-data-for-ai-agents)
or [Amazon](/capabilities/ecommerce/amazon-product-data-for-ai-agents).

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="1688 上搜不锈钢保温杯的货源")
find_tools(q="wholesale suppliers for silicone phone cases on 1688")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/1688")         # browse everything for 1688
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/alibaba")      # Alibaba.com international search
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

1688 prices come back in yuan, as the listing shows them, not converted;
Alibaba.com prices come back as that site shows them. Always `describe_tool`
before executing: it shows the exact parameters, the item ID the detail lookup
expects, and the per-call cost.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** The same capability is available as fixed-contract
  [Scenario Methods](/api-reference/scenario/overview) with automatic provider
  failover:
  [`ecommerce.1688.product.search`](/api-reference/scenario/methods/ecommerce-1688-product-search),
  [`ecommerce.1688.product.get`](/api-reference/scenario/methods/ecommerce-1688-product-get).
  Alibaba.com search is available through the catalog tools above only.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a 1688 or Alibaba account?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Alibaba.com the same as 1688?">
    No. Both belong to the Alibaba group, but 1688 is the domestic Chinese
    wholesale marketplace and Alibaba.com the international one. They have
    separate catalogs and separate tools: browse `ecommerce/1688` for one and
    `ecommerce/alibaba` for the other.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What exactly comes back for a listing?">
    The public detail of the listing as 1688 presents it. Which fields a given
    offer carries depends on the seller, so run `describe_tool` for the
    parameters and try one item before relying on a specific field.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh are prices?">
    Every call goes to 1688 at request time; nothing is served from a stale
    archive. A price is what the listing shows at that moment, in yuan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
