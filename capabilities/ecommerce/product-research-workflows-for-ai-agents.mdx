Each marketplace page in this category describes one platform. Real product
research rarely stays on one: search where the customers are, open the
promising listings, read what buyers say, check what already sells, then
compare across sites. This page shows how an agent chains those steps through
AgentKey — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` at each hop — and
which marketplace supports which step.

## The research loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search by keyword">
    Start where your customers are. Every platform here supports keyword
    search;
    [Amazon](/capabilities/ecommerce/amazon-product-data-for-ai-agents) also accepts an ASIN, and
    [Shopee](/capabilities/ecommerce/shopee-product-data-for-ai-agents) can return category facets for a
    keyword before you drill in, and Google Shopping returns offers from many
    stores in one call. Use sort and price filters to keep the set small.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shortlist">
    Keep the item IDs (or ASINs) worth a closer look, plus the brand and
    model: IDs do not travel between marketplaces, names do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open details and SKUs">
    Pull the product detail for each shortlisted ID. Where variants matter,
    Shopee exposes the SKU matrix and observed SKU models and
    [Douyin Shop](/capabilities/ecommerce/douyin-shop-product-data-for-ai-agents) the SKU information;
    [JD.com](/capabilities/ecommerce/jd-product-data-for-ai-agents) has a price-only lookup for when the
    full listing is more than you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the reviews">
    Amazon returns the top reviews; Shopee adds review tags and the rating
    split per SKU model; [Taobao / Tmall](/capabilities/ecommerce/taobao-and-tmall-product-data-for-ai-agents)
    pairs reviews with buyer questions; JD.com and Douyin Shop return paginated
    comments, and [TikTok Shop](/capabilities/ecommerce/tiktok-shop-product-data-for-ai-agents) product
    reviews. [AliExpress](/capabilities/ecommerce/aliexpress-product-data-for-ai-agents)
    adds buyer reviews with a star distribution, and
    [Walmart](/capabilities/ecommerce/walmart-product-data-for-ai-agents),
    [Target](/capabilities/ecommerce/target-product-data-for-ai-agents) and
    [Best Buy](/capabilities/ecommerce/best-buy-product-data-for-ai-agents)
    return pages of customer reviews. Use a rating filter where one exists to
    go straight to complaints.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what is already selling">
    Amazon's Best Sellers for a category path and its products-by-category
    listing show the incumbents; TikTok Shop's hot-selling list does the same
    for its Southeast Asian, Japanese and Mexican regions, and
    [eBay](/capabilities/ecommerce/ebay-product-data-for-ai-agents) sold listings show what actually sold
    and at what price. Shop product lists on Shopee, Taobao, JD.com, Douyin
    Shop, TikTok Shop, [Etsy](/capabilities/ecommerce/etsy-product-data-for-ai-agents) and any
    [Shopify store](/capabilities/ecommerce/shopify-store-data-for-ai-agents), plus Amazon seller
    storefronts, show what a specific competitor carries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare across platforms">
    Repeat search and detail on the other marketplaces with the brand and
    model, then line the listings up — converting currencies first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prices, deals and services across stores

Three more sources help at the edges of the loop:

- **Google Shopping** — `find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/google-shopping")`. One
  keyword search returns product offers from many stores — title, price,
  store, rating and link — localized by country and language, filtered by
  condition and sorted by price or rating. A quick first pass on who sells a
  product and for how much.
- **Slickdeals** — `find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/slickdeals")`. Deal search by
  keyword with price, minimum-discount and hide-expired filters, sorted by
  relevance, newest, popularity or price — title, price, list price, store,
  community score and comment count — and a single deal's coupon code, views,
  comments and description. Shows which discounts shoppers actually vote up.
- **Fiverr** — `find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/fiverr")`. Gig listings from a
  Fiverr search or category URL — titles, sellers, ratings and pricing — for
  sizing up freelance services such as product photography or store setup the
  same way you size up products.

## Worked examples

*"Find me something to sell: what is moving in kitchen gadgets on Amazon US,
what do buyers complain about, and can I source it wholesale?"*
Amazon Best Sellers for the category path → product detail for the top ASINs
→ top reviews for each → search [1688](/capabilities/ecommerce/1688-wholesale-product-data-for-ai-agents) with the
product's generic name → open the most promising wholesale listings.

*"Is this Shopee Taiwan item cheaper than the same product on Taobao?"*
Shopee item detail and SKU matrix → Taobao search with the brand and model,
Tmall-only if you want official stores → Taobao detail for the best matches →
convert New Taiwan dollars and yuan to one currency, then compare per SKU.

*"What is a fair price for a used Sony A7 III?"*
[eBay](/capabilities/ecommerce/ebay-product-data-for-ai-agents) sold listings for the model, used only
→ active Buy It Now listings for comparison → Google Shopping offers for the
new price → Slickdeals for recent discounts on it.

*"Audit this JD.com shop."*
JD shop product list by shop ID → current price for each item → comments for
the items you care about → a table of price, rating signal and recurring
complaints.

## Comparing one product across Amazon, Shopee and Taobao

The three biggest comparison targets behave differently; expect it:

- **Identify the product by name, not by ID.** An ASIN means nothing on Shopee
  or Taobao. Search each platform with the brand and model, then confirm the
  match from the detail page.
- **Pick the region first.** Amazon takes a country marketplace, Shopee a site,
  Taobao none. The same product on two Amazon marketplaces is two listings
  with two prices.
- **Normalize the currency yourself.** Prices come back as each platform shows
  them — the marketplace's currency on Amazon, the site's on Shopee, yuan on
  Taobao. AgentKey does not convert.
- **Variants are not aligned.** Shopee's SKU matrix, Taobao's item page and
  Amazon's listing can split one product into different options; compare at
  the option level when price per unit matters.
- **Review and ranking signals differ in shape.** Amazon gives top reviews and
  Best Sellers; Shopee gives reviews with tags and a per-model split; Taobao
  gives reviews plus buyer questions, and a seller's list sorted by sales is
  its closest thing to a ranking. Summarize each in its own terms first.

## Building it in code

On the platforms that have them, every step above maps to a
[Scenario Method](/api-reference/scenario/overview) with a fixed contract and
automatic provider failover — `product.search`,
`product.get`, `product.reviews`, `product.rank`, `shop.products` — each
scoped to one platform and returning a normalized Product or Review page, so a
pipeline can send one request shape to Amazon, Shopee and Taobao and merge the
results without per-platform parsing. Each platform page lists its methods.
eBay, Etsy, Shopify stores, the US retailers, ASOS, the app stores and review
sites, Alibaba.com, Google Shopping, Slickdeals and Fiverr are available
through the catalog tools only for now.
