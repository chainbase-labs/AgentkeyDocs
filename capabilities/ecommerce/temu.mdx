Temu merchandises through its homepage: the feed is where new arrivals, deep
discounts and the products it wants to push appear first, and it changes
constantly. There is no public API, and the site is built to be used in the
app. Through AgentKey your agent reads that feed the same way it reads
everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — choosing
the Temu site on each call.

## What you can ask

- *"What is on Temu's US homepage right now? List the first 40 products with prices."*
- *"Pull the Temu homepage feed for the UK site and tell me the price range and the most common product types."*
- *"Fetch Temu's homepage feed every morning and tell me which products are new since yesterday."*

## What's covered

**Homepage feed**
- Product cards from the Temu homepage for a selected site, with offset
  pagination — the products Temu is actively pushing at that moment
- Suited to product sourcing, catalog monitoring and marketplace trend
  analysis

**Sites**
- The feed is fetched per site, so the same request can be repeated across
  Temu's regional storefronts

Not covered today: keyword search, product detail by ID, reviews and shop
pages for Temu. For a product you spot in the feed, search the same keyword
on [Amazon](/capabilities/ecommerce/amazon),
[AliExpress](/capabilities/ecommerce/aliexpress) or
[1688](/capabilities/ecommerce/1688) to go deeper.

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="what Temu is promoting on its US homepage today")
find_tools(q="Temu 美国站首页在推什么商品")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/temu")         # browse everything for Temu
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Prices come back in the currency of the site you selected, not converted.
Always `describe_tool` before executing: it shows the site field and its
accepted values, the offset parameter, and the per-call cost.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Temu account?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I search Temu by keyword or open a single product?">
    Not today. The Temu coverage is the homepage feed; use it to see what the
    platform is pushing, then continue the research on a marketplace with
    search and detail lookups.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh is the feed?">
    Every call goes to Temu at request time; nothing is served from a stale
    archive. The cards are what the homepage shows for that site at that
    moment, so two calls minutes apart can differ.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
