Douyin's shop lives inside the app, where products are sold through short
video and live streams, and it is one of the most closed marketplaces an agent
can face: no public catalog API, and nothing rendered on the open web without
an app session. Through AgentKey your agent reads public Douyin E-commerce
data the same way it reads everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`,
`execute_tool` — from a keyword search down to the SKU list of one item.

## What you can ask

- *"抖音商城搜 '洗地机'，列出前 20 个商品和价格。"*
- *"这个抖音商品有哪些 SKU 规格，分别多少钱？"*
- *"Summarize the buyer comments on this Douyin item."*
- *"List the products in this Douyin shop, page by page."*
- *"Search Douyin Shop for 'camping tent' and keep paging until you have 50 results."*
- *"Compare this Douyin listing's SKUs and price with the same product on Taobao."*

## What's covered

**Search**
- Keyword search with page and search-ID pagination, so a multi-page search
  continues from where it stopped

**Product details & SKUs**
- Item details by item ID — inspect a known marketplace item
- SKU information for an item — the purchasable variations and their options

**Comments**
- Paginated customer comments for an item by item ID

**Shops**
- A paginated product list for a shop by shop ID — browse a known seller's
  catalog page by page

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="抖音商城这个商品的 SKU 规格和价格")
find_tools(q="buyer comments on this Douyin Shop item")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/douyin-ec")    # browse everything for Douyin Shop
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Prices come back in yuan, as the listing shows them, not converted. Aliases
resolve (抖音商城, Douyin Shop and Douyin E-commerce all land here).
Always `describe_tool` before executing: some operations are listed in more
than one version, and it shows the exact parameters, the identifier expected
(item ID or shop ID) and the per-call cost.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Douyin account or shop access?">
    No. AgentKey holds the upstream access; your agent only needs your AgentKey
    subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it read live-stream prices or creator-only deals?">
    Only what the public product page carries: the item's details, SKUs,
    comments and the shop's product list. Prices tied to a live session are
    not a separate feed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh are prices and comments?">
    Every call goes to the platform at request time; nothing is served from a
    stale archive. A price is what the item shows at that moment, in yuan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
