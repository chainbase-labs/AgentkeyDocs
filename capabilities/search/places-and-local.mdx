"Where is the nearest…" and "what is the best… near…" are among the most common
questions an assistant gets, and answering them needs a live business index,
not a model's memory. Through AgentKey your agent searches places with the
same three calls — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — using
Google Places and Maps results through Serper, or Brave's place search with
its point-of-interest details and generated descriptions.

## What you can ask

- *"Find coffee shops near Shibuya station."*
- *"List pharmacies within 2 km of this latitude and longitude."*
- *"What are the top ramen places on Google Maps around this coordinate at zoom 14?"*
- *"Give me the address, phone number and website of the Apple Store on Regent Street."*
- *"Describe these three restaurants from the search results in one sentence each."*
- *"Search for dentists around 'Austin, Texas, United States', second page."*
- *"Which businesses, streets and addresses match 'Marienplatz' in Munich?"*

## What's covered

**Google Places and Maps results**
- Places by keyword, biased by a country code, interface language and a
  free-text location such as "New York, United States"
- Maps results centred on a latitude, longitude and zoom level
- Result count, page numbers and a time filter

**Brave place search**
- Places, businesses, cities, addresses and streets around a location, given
  as a latitude / longitude with a radius or as a named location
- A country and result language, metric or imperial units, and a per-call
  count

**Brave point-of-interest details**
- Extra information for up to 20 points of interest in one request
- AI-generated descriptions for places
- Both take location ids from a Brave web search run with the locations
  result filter; the ids are short-lived, so fetch and use them in the same
  session

**What comes back**
- Each place as the engine serves it: name, address and position, with any
  ratings, categories, hours or contact details the engine attaches

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the engine:

```text
find_tools(q="coffee shops near Shibuya station with ratings")
find_tools(q="businesses and streets around this latitude and longitude")
find_tools(prefix="search/serper")        # Google Places and Maps results
find_tools(prefix="search/brave")         # Brave place search and POI details
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Always `describe_tool` before executing — Google Maps results take the centre
as `ll` in the form `@lat,lng,zoomz`, Brave takes `latitude`, `longitude` and
`radius`, and the POI tools need ids from an earlier Brave web search.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which regions are covered?">
    Google results accept any country code and a free-text location anywhere
    Google Maps has coverage. Brave place search takes a fixed list of
    countries across the Americas, Europe and Asia-Pacific, including China,
    Hong Kong, Taiwan, Japan and Korea.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh is the data?">
    Every call goes to the engine at request time. Brave POI ids are
    short-lived and belong to the search that produced them, so do not store
    them between sessions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it give directions or travel times?">
    No. These tools find and describe places; they do not route between them.
    For taxi quotes between two places see
    [Attractions and local transport](/capabilities/travel/attractions).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
