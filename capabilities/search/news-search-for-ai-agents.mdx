News is where an agent most often needs something the model has never seen:
what happened this morning, what a company announced an hour ago, how a story
is being covered in another country. Through AgentKey your agent searches
headlines with the same three calls it uses everywhere — `find_tools`,
`describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — choosing between Google News results through
Serper, Brave's own news index and Tavily's news topic, which can also return
the article text.

## What you can ask

- *"What are the top headlines on the Fed rate decision in the last 24 hours?"*
- *"Find news about lithium mining from UK sources this week."*
- *"Any news on Anthropic published in the past hour?"*
- *"Show me Japanese-language coverage of the Nikkei record high."*
- *"Search news published between 1 and 15 September about the EU AI Act."*
- *"Give me 50 recent news results about Tesla and group them by outlet."*
- *"Pull this week's news on the typhoon in Guangdong with the article text, and a short summary."*
- *"Get page two of the news results for this query."*

## What's covered

**Headlines by keyword**
- Google News results with title, link, source, date and snippet
- Brave news articles from its own index, with optional extra snippets and
  Goggles re-ranking
- Tavily's news topic, tuned for real-time updates, with each article's
  cleaned content as markdown or plain text on request and an optional
  generated answer

**Recency**
- Past hour, day, week, month or year on Google News
- The last 24 hours, 7 days, 31 days or 365 days, or a custom date range, on
  Brave
- A relative time range or explicit start and end dates on Tavily

**Region and language**
- Country and interface-language codes, plus a free-text location, on Google
  News
- Country, result language and interface language on Brave, with optional
  latitude / longitude, city or postal-code hints
- Domain include and exclude lists on Tavily to pin or drop specific outlets

**Volume and paging**
- A per-call result count on every engine; page numbers on Google News and an
  offset on Brave
- A POST variant of Brave news for long or multi-line queries

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the engine:

```text
find_tools(q="latest news about Anthropic from the past week")
find_tools(q="Japanese coverage of the Nikkei record high today")
find_tools(prefix="search/serper")        # Google News results
find_tools(prefix="search/brave")         # Brave news index
find_tools(prefix="search/tavily")        # news topic with article text
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Always `describe_tool` before executing — the recency filter is `tbs` on
Google News, `freshness` on Brave and `time_range` or dates on Tavily, and
per-call costs differ.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** News search is part of the fixed-contract
  [`web.search`](/api-reference/scenario/methods/web-search) Scenario Method:
  set `type` to `news` and it returns a normalized result page with automatic
  engine failover. See [Scenario Methods](/api-reference/scenario/overview).
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How fresh are the headlines?">
    Every call goes to the engine at request time. The recency filters narrow
    by the engine's own publication or discovery date, down to the past hour
    on Google News and the past 24 hours on Brave.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which regions and languages?">
    Google News takes any Google country and interface-language code; Brave
    takes its country and language lists; Tavily is scoped by domain lists
    rather than by country. Queries can be written in any language.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I get the full article?">
    Google News and Brave return headline, source, date and snippet with the
    link. Tavily can include each article's cleaned content. For any other
    link, pass the URL to a [scrape tool](/capabilities/scrape/url-to-markdown-for-ai-agents)
    to read the page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
