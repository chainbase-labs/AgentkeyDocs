Job postings are spread across thousands of career sites and boards, each with
its own search form and anti-scraping measures. Through AgentKey your agent
searches them in one call — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` —
with JSearch behind the scenes for the Google for Jobs aggregate, which indexes
jobs posted on any public job site, and Liepin for the China market.

## What you can ask

- *"Find remote senior backend engineer jobs posted in the last week."*
- *"Data analyst roles in Austin, full-time only, with a salary listed."*
- *"Which companies are hiring product managers in Berlin right now?"*
- *"Show me the next page of results."*
- *"Entry-level marketing jobs in Toronto that don't require a degree."*
- *"上海的 Go 后端开发岗位，猎聘上有哪些？"*
- *"Compare how many openings mention 'LLM' in London versus New York."*

## What's covered

**Global job search**
- Search jobs posted on any public job site across the web, aggregated by
  Google for Jobs
- Every filter Google for Jobs offers — the full set of its options is
  exposed
- Pagination through result pages

**China (Liepin)**
- Search Liepin postings with a filter object in which every field is
  optional; an empty filter returns unfiltered results
- Read-only and safe to retry; zero-based page numbering

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="remote senior backend engineer jobs posted this week")
find_tools(q="上海的 Go 后端开发岗位")
find_tools(prefix="jobs/jsearch")            # Google for Jobs aggregate
find_tools(prefix="jobs/liepin")             # China
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work. Always `describe_tool` before
executing — the filter parameters are extensive and differ between the two
providers, as do per-call costs.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I filter by remote, date posted or employment type?">
    Yes. The global search exposes all the options Google for Jobs offers,
    including those; `describe_tool` lists the exact parameter names and
    accepted values.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do search results include how to apply?">
    Search returns the postings themselves. For application options and
    links, employer reviews and salary context on one posting, open it with
    the details tool — see
    [Job details & salaries](/capabilities/jobs/job-details-and-salary-data-for-ai-agents).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which sites are covered?">
    Any public job site that Google for Jobs indexes, worldwide. Liepin
    postings are covered by the China route.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
