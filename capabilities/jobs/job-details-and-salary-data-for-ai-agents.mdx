A search result tells an agent that a job exists; the next questions are how
to apply, what the employer is like and whether the pay is fair. Through
AgentKey your agent opens a posting and pulls salary estimates with the same
`find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` loop, with JSearch behind the
scenes.

## What you can ask

- *"Open this posting and give me every way to apply."*
- *"What do employees say about this employer?"*
- *"What does a data engineer make in Seattle — hourly and yearly?"*
- *"Estimated pay for a senior software engineer at Google in New York with eight years of experience."*
- *"Compare product manager salaries in London, Berlin and Amsterdam."*
- *"Is this posting's salary above the estimate for similar jobs nearby?"*

## What's covered

**Posting details**
- All details of one job posting, including application options and links
- Employer reviews
- Estimated salaries for similar jobs

**Salary estimates by location**
- Estimated pay for a job title around a location
- Returned for several periods as the data allows — hourly, daily, weekly,
  monthly or yearly

**Salary estimates by company**
- Estimated pay at a specific company by job title
- Optional location and experience level in years

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="details and application links for this job posting")
find_tools(q="data engineer salary in Seattle")
find_tools(prefix="jobs/jsearch")            # details, salary by location, salary by company
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Always `describe_tool` before executing — the details tool takes a job id from
a search result, and the two salary tools take different combinations of
title, location, company and experience.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where does the job id come from?">
    From a search result — see [Job search](/capabilities/jobs/job-search-for-ai-agents).
    Run the search first, then pass the id of the posting you want to open.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are the salaries actual or estimated?">
    Estimated. Both salary tools return estimates for a title in a location or
    at a company, and the periods returned (hourly through yearly) depend on
    what data is available and relevant for that query.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I estimate pay by seniority?">
    Yes — the company salary tool accepts an experience level in years
    alongside the title and an optional location.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
