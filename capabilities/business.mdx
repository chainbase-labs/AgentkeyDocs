The **Business** category gives agents structured company intelligence from two
providers: global startup and venture data from Crunchbase, and crypto projects,
funds and investors from RootData. Typical flow: resolve a name to an
identifier, then fetch the detailed record.

## Providers

| Provider | Coverage |
| --- | --- |
| **Crunchbase** | Lookup and search of every Crunchbase entity type: organizations, people, funding rounds, investments, acquisitions, IPOs, funds, events, jobs, layoffs, key-employee changes, legal proceedings, products and launches, ownerships, press references, awards, categories and locations; prediction and insight entities (funding, acquisition, IPO, growth, closure); `autocompletes` resolves a name to an entity identifier |
| **RootData** | Crypto projects, VCs / investors and people: full profiles, fundraising rounds, ecosystems and tags, calendar events, hires and departures, Hot and Growth indices, X (Twitter) follow graph, trending projects on X, perpetual-exchange leaderboard |

Tool names follow the provider: `Crunchbase/searchOrganizations`,
`RootData/getProject`. The per-call cost is shown by `describe_tool`.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** Developers and enterprise integrations can call this
  category through [Scenario Methods](/api-reference/scenario/overview): fixed
  REST contracts with a stable schema, a fixed price and automatic provider
  failover. The methods are listed by platform in the API Reference sidebar.
</Note>

## Browse the catalog

```text
find_tools(prefix="business")
find_tools(q="funding rounds raised by Anthropic")
describe_tool("Crunchbase/searchFundingRounds")
execute_tool("Crunchbase/searchFundingRounds", { ...params })   # copy describe_tool's execute_as
```

<Tip>
  Resolve the entity first — `Crunchbase/autocompletes` turns a name into the
  identifier the detail tools expect; RootData lookups take its own project and
  people ids, which `business.company.search` returns.
</Tip>
