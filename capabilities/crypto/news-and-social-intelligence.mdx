Crypto moves on information: a headline, a listing, a shift in who is talking
about a project on X. An agent that wants that signal has to read news sites,
social analytics and event calendars that are each sold separately. Through
AgentKey your agent reads all of it the same way — `find_tools`,
`describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — with PANews, Surf and CoinMarketCap behind
the scenes, from a raw article feed to a project's sentiment score.

## What you can ask

- *"What are the top crypto stories of the last 24 hours?"*
- *"Summarize today's must-read articles on PANews."*
- *"What is the social sentiment around Solana this week, and is its mindshare rising?"*
- *"Which DEX projects have the most mindshare over the last 7 days?"*
- *"Who are the smart followers of this project's X account — VCs, KOLs or developers?"*
- *"Any exchange listings or delistings this week?"*
- *"Which airdrops are live right now, and what tasks do they require?"*
- *"最近有哪些值得关注的行业活动和会议？"*

## What's covered

**News**
- Latest articles, filterable by column or series; a cross-source crypto news
  feed filterable by source, project and time range, sorted by recency or
  trending
- Full article content by id
- 24-hour hot article ranking, 7-day search trending ranking and daily
  must-reads
- Series and topics — list, search and detail with latest comments
- Keyword search of articles, ranked by relevance with highlighted matching
  fragments
- AI-generated news and signal analysis per project: title, summary, TL;DR,
  signal type (price analysis, mindshare, funding, Twitter), source URLs and
  source tweet

**Social intelligence on X (Twitter)**
- Project-level X analytics: sentiment score from -1 (bearish) to 1
  (bullish), follower geography, smart-follower breakdown into VCs, KOLs and
  developers
- Mindshare — social view count — as a ranking of projects scoped by tag
  (dex, l1, meme) and window from 24h to 30d, and as a time series per project
- Engagement score for an account on a 0–1000 scale; smart follower count
  history for a project
- X profile, followers, following, recent posts (with or without retweets) and
  replies for a handle; posts by id; replies to a tweet
- Search X posts by keyword or `from:handle`; search X users by keyword

**Industry events**
- Calendar events and categories; industry events and activities
- Exchange listing and delisting events, filterable by date range, exchange,
  token symbol, product type and event type
- Token generation event status and public sale (IDO/ICO/IEO) details — sale
  type, status, price, raise amount and ROI
- Airdrops past, present and future; airdrop search by keyword, status, reward
  type and task type; airdrop activities with social metrics

**Cross-cutting search**
- Projects, funds, wallets (by ENS name, label or address prefix), prediction
  markets, and project events of type launch, upgrade, partnership, news,
  airdrop, listing or twitter
- Web search over pages and articles, optionally restricted to one site

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="crypto news about Solana in the last 24 hours")
find_tools(q="以太坊最近的社交情绪和 mindshare")
find_tools(prefix="crypto/news")          # feeds, articles, rankings, topics
find_tools(prefix="crypto/intel")         # sentiment, mindshare, smart followers
find_tools(prefix="crypto/events")        # calendar, listings, TGE, airdrops
find_tools(prefix="crypto/search")        # cross-cutting keyword search
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Both English and Chinese phrasing work. Always `describe_tool` before
executing — social tools look up by handle, X id or project name depending on
the tool, and per-call costs differ between providers.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** The same capability is available as a fixed-contract

  [`crypto.news.search`](/api-reference/scenario/methods/crypto-news-search)

  given.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which news sources are included?">
    PANews articles, columns, series and topics, plus a feed that aggregates
    major crypto news sources and can be filtered to one source. Social
    analytics cover X (Twitter) only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do sentiment and mindshare mean here?">
    Sentiment is an aggregate score for a project on a -1 to 1 scale.
    Mindshare is social view count, available as a cross-project ranking and
    as a per-project time series. Smart followers are the subset of a
    project's X followers classified as VCs, KOLs or developers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is historical data available?">
    Yes — mindshare and smart-follower history accept a date range, the news
    feed and listing events filter by time range, and the calendar covers
    scheduled events. Everything is fetched at request time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
