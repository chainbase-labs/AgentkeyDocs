The **E-commerce** category lets agents read marketplaces the way a shopper
does: search listings by keyword, open a product page, read its reviews and SKU
options, list what a shop sells, and pull best-seller rankings —
across global and Chinese platforms.

## Platforms

Catalog tools in this category come from **JustOneAPI**, named
`JustOneAPI/getApi<Platform><Operation>V<n>`; several platforms expose more
than one version of the same operation. Global marketplaces are listed first,
then the Chinese ones; each has its own page, and
[Product research workflows](/capabilities/ecommerce/product-research) shows
how to chain them.

### Global marketplaces

| Platform | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| [Amazon](/capabilities/ecommerce/amazon) | Product search, product details, top reviews, best sellers, products by category |
| [Shopee](/capabilities/ecommerce/shopee) | Item search and search facets; item details, display snapshot, SKU matrix and models, installments; item reviews, review tags and model distribution; shop profile, details, categories, item list and reviews |
| [AliExpress](/capabilities/ecommerce/aliexpress) | Product search, product details |
| [TikTok Shop](/capabilities/ecommerce/tiktok-shop) | Product search, product details; reviews, seller product lists and hot sellers from TikHub, filed under TikTok |

### China marketplaces

| Platform | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| [Taobao / Tmall](/capabilities/ecommerce/taobao-tmall) | Product search, product details, product reviews, product Q&A, shop product list |
| [JD.com](/capabilities/ecommerce/jd) | Product search, details, price, comments, shop product list |
| [Douyin E-commerce](/capabilities/ecommerce/douyin-shop) | Product search, item details, SKU info, item comments, shop product list |
| [1688](/capabilities/ecommerce/1688) | Product search, product details |
| [Xianyu](/capabilities/ecommerce/xianyu) | Product search, product details |
| [Xiaohongshu E-commerce](/capabilities/ecommerce/xiaohongshu-shop) | Product search |

The per-call cost is shown by `describe_tool`. Prices come back in the
platform's own currency, and several marketplaces take a `country` or site
parameter — check `describe_tool` for the exact fields.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** Developers and enterprise integrations can call this
  category through [Scenario Methods](/api-reference/scenario/overview): fixed
  REST contracts with a stable schema, a fixed price and automatic provider
  failover. The methods are listed by platform in the API Reference sidebar.
</Note>

## Browse the catalog

```text
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce")
find_tools(prefix="ecommerce/taobao")         # one marketplace's subtree
find_tools(q="search Amazon for wireless earbuds under 50 dollars")
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })   # copy describe_tool's execute_as
```

<Note>
  Marketplaces differ in which fields a product carries and which region or
  site a tool targets — check `describe_tool` for the exact parameters.
  `execute_tool` calls exactly the tool you name; if you want AgentKey to pick
  the route and fail over for you, use a Scenario Method.
</Note>
