Once the flight and the hotel are settled, the rest of the trip is what to do
and how to get there — tours and tickets, their reviews and free dates, a taxi
from the airport — plus the small facts every travel agent needs: which
currencies and languages exist, today's exchange rate, the coordinates of an
address. Through AgentKey your agent gets all of this from Booking.com with
the same three calls — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool`. Every
operation is a lookup; nothing is booked.

## What you can ask

- *"What are the top-rated things to do in Barcelona next weekend?"*
- *"Find museum tours in Kyoto sorted by lowest price."*
- *"Show the details and recent reviews for this food tour."*
- *"Which dates in May is this tour available?"*
- *"What attractions are popular near this hotel?"*
- *"How much is a taxi from Heathrow to King's Cross at nine on Friday morning?"*
- *"罗马有哪些热门景点和一日游？"*
- *"Convert this price to Japanese yen at today's rate."*

## What's covered

**Attractions and tours**
- Location search by city, district or place name, returning destination ids
  and product slugs
- Attraction search for a destination, sorted by trending, booking score or
  lowest price, with type, price, label and area filters discovered from the
  search response, an optional date window, currency, language and paging
- Attraction details by product slug; reviews with user ratings, comments and
  review counts
- Availability of a product on a date, and its availability calendar
- Popular attractions near a hotel

**Taxis**
- Place search for pick-up and drop-off points, returning place ids
- Taxi search between two places for a pick-up date and 24-hour time, priced
  in a chosen currency and described in a chosen language

**Reference data**
- The list of available currencies; exchange rates for every currency against
  a base currency
- The list of response languages; the list of supported locations
- Any address or place name turned into latitude and longitude

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="top-rated things to do in Barcelona this weekend")
find_tools(q="罗马热门景点和一日游")
find_tools(prefix="travel/booking")        # browse all Booking.com tools
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

The tools chain: attraction location search gives an id for the search and a
slug for details, availability and reviews; taxi place search gives the place
ids the taxi search takes. Always `describe_tool` before executing — the
filter values for attractions come back inside the search response.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** Attractions, taxis and the reference lookups are
  available through the catalog tools above; there is no fixed-contract
  [Scenario Method](/api-reference/scenario/overview) for them yet. Hotel and
  flight search do have methods — see [Hotels](/capabilities/travel/hotel-search-for-ai-agents)
  and [Flights](/capabilities/travel/flight-search-for-ai-agents).
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does it buy tickets or book the taxi?">
    No. Attraction availability and taxi search return options and prices
    only; no ticket is purchased and no ride is booked.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How current are prices and availability?">
    Fetched at request time. Exchange rates are whatever Booking.com serves
    when you call; attraction availability and taxi quotes should be
    re-checked before the user commits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which languages and currencies?">
    Any language and currency on Booking.com's own lists, which are
    themselves available here as lookups — fetch them once and reuse the codes
    across hotel, flight, car and attraction calls.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
