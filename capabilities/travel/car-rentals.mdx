Renting a car has more moving parts than a hotel night — pick-up and drop-off
points and times, driver age, car category, supplier reputation, the fees that
appear at the counter. Through AgentKey your agent works through all of them
against Booking.com car-rental data with the same three calls — `find_tools`,
`describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — up to and including the final price recap.
It never places the reservation.

## What you can ask

- *"Rent a mid-size car at Lisbon airport, 12 to 15 November, pick-up at ten in the morning."*
- *"Compare Avis and Budget prices for an SUV at JFK next weekend."*
- *"What does this car cost in total with fees, and where exactly is the pick-up desk?"*
- *"How is this supplier rated, and what do recent renters say?"*
- *"Are automatic cars available in Palermo for a 24-year-old driver?"*
- *"巴黎戴高乐机场租车三天，自动挡，大概多少钱？"*
- *"Give me the final price recap for this offer before I decide."*

## What's covered

**Pick-up and drop-off locations**
- Autocomplete search for cities and airports, and search by name, address,
  city, state or country, returning the coordinates the rental search needs

**Rental search**
- Available cars for pick-up and drop-off coordinates, dates and 24-hour
  times, and a driver age between 20 and 65
- Filters by supplier, car category and the other facets the search itself
  returns, applied together
- Results as a feed of car cards with result counts and the available filter
  facets; prices in a chosen currency and text in a chosen language
- One of the search flows supports pick-up and drop-off at the same location
  only

**Vehicle and offer details**
- The full detail page for one offer: specification, price breakdown, fees,
  pick-up and drop-off depot information, and the link to the rental terms
- Package options available for an offer

**Supplier information**
- Supplier details for an offer — rating breakdown, opening hours, pick-up
  instructions, legal and contact information
- Supplier ratings and the customer review list

**Price recap**
- The "confirm your deal" summary for an offer: vehicle, pick-up and
  drop-off, and the full price breakdown — the last step before checkout,
  which stays with the user

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="rent a mid-size car at Lisbon airport 12 to 15 November")
find_tools(q="巴黎戴高乐机场租车三天 自动挡")
find_tools(prefix="travel/booking")        # browse all Booking.com tools
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

The tools chain: location search gives coordinates, the rental search gives a
search key plus a vehicle id for each car, and the vehicle, supplier and
summary tools take that pair. Always `describe_tool` before executing — the
search requires dates, times and both sets of coordinates.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** Car rentals are available through the catalog tools
  above; there is no fixed-contract
  [Scenario Method](/api-reference/scenario/overview) for them yet. Hotel and
  flight search do have methods — see [Hotels](/capabilities/travel/hotels)
  and [Flights](/capabilities/travel/flights).
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does it reserve the car?">
    No. Even the booking summary is a price recap, not a booking: it shows the
    vehicle, pick-up, drop-off and the full price breakdown so the user can
    decide. No reservation or payment is made.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How current are the prices?">
    Fetched at request time for the dates, times and driver age you give. The
    search key ties the detail tools to that search, so re-run the search if
    a details call says the key is stale.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which currencies, languages and locations?">
    Prices in any currency on Booking.com's currency list and text in any
    language on its language list; pick-up points anywhere its car-rental
    partners operate, found through the location search.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
