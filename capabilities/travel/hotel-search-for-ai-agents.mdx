Finding a hotel is a conversation — a city, dates, who is travelling, a
budget, then the questions a guest actually asks: what are the rooms like, is
breakfast included, what do recent guests say, can children stay. Through
AgentKey your agent runs that whole conversation against Booking.com data —
with Google Hotels to compare what each booking site charges and Airbnb for
holiday rentals — using the same three calls: `find_tools`, `describe_tool`,
`execute_tool`. Every
operation is a lookup: your agent searches, prices and reads, and never places
a reservation.

## What you can ask

- *"Find hotels in Lisbon for two adults, 12 to 15 November, under 150 euros a night, sorted by review score."*
- *"Which hotels within 5 km of the Colosseum are available 3 to 6 May?"*
- *"Show me the room types and prices at this hotel for two adults and a three-year-old."*
- *"What are the check-in times and the cancellation and pet policies here?"*
- *"Pull the latest reviews and the score breakdown for this property."*
- *"东京新宿附近，12 月 24 到 26 日，两大一小，有哪些酒店？"*
- *"Does this hotel take cards, or is payment at the property?"*
- *"Is there any night between 1 and 30 March with a room free at this hotel?"*
- *"On Google Hotels, what does each booking site charge for this hotel for 3 to 5 April?"*
- *"Find an Airbnb in Lisbon for four adults with at least two bedrooms that allows pets."*

## What's covered

**Finding the destination**
- Destination search by city, district, landmark, region or country name,
  returning the destination id and type the hotel search needs
- An address or place name turned into latitude and longitude; cities near a
  coordinate for side trips

**Hotel search**
- By destination for check-in and check-out dates, number of adults, each
  child's age and number of rooms
- Around a coordinate with a radius from 10 to 500 km
- Minimum and maximum price, category filters and sort orders, with the valid
  filter and sort values available from their own lookups
- Currency, response language, metric or imperial distances, Celsius or
  Fahrenheit, and page numbers

**Property details**
- Full details for your dates and party; description and general
  information; facilities; photos
- Policies; payment features; children and extra-bed policies

**Rooms and availability**
- The room list, and the room list with live availability and prices for the
  stay
- Availability across a date range for a hotel, to find free nights

**Reviews and Q&A**
- The review-score breakdown; guest reviews and tips with sort options and
  paging; the filter metadata for reviews
- Questions guests have asked the property, with the answers

**Price comparison on Google Hotels**
- Hotel search by destination or landmark for your dates, adults and each
  child's age — Google property ID, nightly price, rating, review count,
  coordinates, star class and photos — in a chosen currency
- One hotel by property ID — address, phone, website, rating, nightly price,
  the price from each booking site, amenities and check-in times

**Holiday rentals**
- Airbnb listing search by location and dates for adults, children, infants
  and pets, with a price range and minimum beds, bedrooms and bathrooms, in a
  chosen currency — name, total-stay price label, rating and host

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="hotels in Lisbon for two adults, 12 to 15 November")
find_tools(q="东京新宿附近 12 月 24 到 26 日的酒店")
find_tools(prefix="travel/booking")        # browse all Booking.com tools
find_tools(prefix="travel/google-hotels")  # also travel/airbnb
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

The tools chain: destination search gives a destination id, the hotel search
gives hotel ids, and the detail, room, policy and review tools take a hotel
id. Google Hotels search returns the property ID its detail tool takes, and
the Google Hotels and Airbnb searches take a place name directly. Always
`describe_tool` before executing — it shows which id each tool expects and the
date format it wants.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** The same capability is available as fixed-contract
  [Scenario Methods](/api-reference/scenario/overview):
  [`travel.hotel.search`](/api-reference/scenario/methods/travel-hotel-search)
  searches hotels around a coordinate for dates and guests and returns a
  normalized hotel page, and
  [`travel.location.search`](/api-reference/scenario/methods/travel-location-search)
  finds the destination first. Google Hotels and Airbnb are available through
  the catalog tools above only.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does it book the room?">
    No. Every operation is read-only — search, prices, availability, details
    and reviews. No reservation, hold or payment is ever made.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which currencies and languages?">
    Prices can be requested in any currency on Booking.com's currency list and
    text in any language on its language list; both lists are available as
    lookups (see
    [Attractions and local transport](/capabilities/travel/attractions-and-local-transport-for-ai-agents)).
    Distances and temperatures can be metric or imperial.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it compare prices across booking sites?">
    Yes. A Google Hotels detail lookup returns the nightly price each booking
    site offers for the hotel and dates you give, alongside the hotel's own
    details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How current are prices and availability?">
    Fetched at request time for the dates and party you give. Rates and
    availability change constantly, so re-run the search before quoting a
    final price.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
