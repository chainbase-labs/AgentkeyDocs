Everything a US public company must disclose is on EDGAR, but EDGAR is built
for humans with a browser: no structured search across forms, no clean way to
pull one section of a 10-K, no normalized insider or 13F tables. Through
AgentKey your agent queries the filings as data — `find_tools`,
`describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — with sec-api's structured datasets, the
SEC's own EDGAR endpoints, and Finnhub and AlphaVantage ownership feeds behind
the scenes.

## What you can ask

- *"Find Apple's latest 10-K and give me the Risk Factors section as plain text."*
- *"Which companies mentioned 'generative AI' in an 8-K this year?"*
- *"List every Form 4 insider sale at Nvidia in the last 90 days."*
- *"What did Berkshire Hathaway hold at the end of last quarter, per its 13F?"*
- *"Who has crossed 5% ownership in this small cap recently?"*
- *"How much was each named executive at Tesla paid last year?"*
- *"Who sits on Boeing's board, and which committees are they on?"*
- *"Has the SEC brought enforcement actions against this investment adviser?"*

## What's covered

**Filings search and documents**
- Search every EDGAR filing from 1993 to present by ticker, CIK, form type,
  filing date and more; results carry accession number, period of report,
  primary-document and exhibit URLs
- Full-text search across filings and their exhibits (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, 20-F,
  S-1 and more) since 2001, narrowable by form type, filer, SIC code and date
- Extract one section — Risk Factors, MD&A, Business — from a 10-K, 10-Q or
  8-K as plain text or the original HTML
- Structured 8-K material events with item numbers and event descriptions
- Company EDGAR profile and filing history by CIK; CIK lookup by name or
  ticker; cross-mapping of CIK, ticker, CUSIP and name; the EDGAR entity master

**XBRL financial data**
- XBRL from a 10-K or 10-Q converted into normalized JSON statements —
  income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and disclosures
- Every reported value of one XBRL concept for a company across its filings;
  one concept across every filer for a calendar period, for screening

**Insider and institutional ownership**
- Insider transactions from Forms 3, 4 and 5 — one route covers US, UK,
  Canada, Australia, India and major EU markets; another covers Form 4 for
  US-traded companies
- Insider sentiment for US companies; Form 144 intent-to-sell notices
- Form 13F institutional holdings and cover pages; institutional holders of a
  US-traded company
- Schedule 13D / 13G beneficial-ownership filings at the 5% threshold
- Fund portfolio holdings (N-PORT), fund census (N-CEN) and proxy-voting
  records (N-PX)
- Congressional trading activity for a company

**Governance and compensation**
- Executive compensation — salary, bonus, stock and option awards, non-equity
  incentive, total — by search or by ticker; director compensation where
  reported
- Directors and board members with roles, committees, independence status and
  term dates
- Auditor and audit-fee disclosures; Exhibit 21 subsidiaries with
  jurisdictions; public float and share count

**Offerings and registrations**
- S-1 / 424B4 prospectuses for IPOs and follow-ons; Form D private placements;
  Form C crowdfunding; Regulation A offerings and their 1-A, 1-K and 1-Z forms
- Investment adviser registrations (Form ADV firms, individuals, brochures and
  ownership schedules); broker-dealer FOCUS reports

**Enforcement**
- SEC enforcement actions, litigation releases, administrative proceedings,
  accounting-and-auditing enforcement releases and SRO rule filings

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="risk factors section of Apple's latest 10-K")
find_tools(q="伯克希尔最新的 13F 持仓")
find_tools(prefix="finance/secapi")          # structured EDGAR datasets, section extractor, XBRL
find_tools(prefix="finance/sec")             # EDGAR submissions, XBRL concepts and frames, full text
find_tools(prefix="finance/finnhub")         # filings list, insider transactions and sentiment
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Name the form ("10-K", "13F", "Form D") and the router ranks the matching
dataset first. Always `describe_tool` before executing — several datasets take
a Lucene query string, and field names and per-call costs differ between
providers.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a CIK?">
    Not to start. Look a company up by name or ticker to get its CIK, or use
    the identifier mapping to go from ticker, CUSIP or name to the full
    identifier set. Many datasets also accept a ticker directly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How far back do filings go?">
    Filing metadata search covers 1993 to present; full-text search covers
    filings and exhibits since 2001. XBRL-based data exists only for filings
    that carry XBRL.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is it only US companies?">
    EDGAR includes foreign private issuers (20-F filers) and every registered
    fund and adviser. Insider-transaction coverage on one route extends to the
    UK, Canada, Australia, India and major EU markets.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
