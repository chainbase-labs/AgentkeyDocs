China's onshore market has its own identifiers, adjustment conventions and
disclosure formats, and nearly all of it is documented in Chinese. Through
AgentKey your agent reads the full Tushare catalog — A-shares, Hong Kong,
indices, funds, bonds, futures, options, FX, gold and China macro — with the
same `find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` loop it uses for US data,
phrasing the request in either language.

## What you can ask

- *"贵州茅台最近一年的日线行情和复权因子"*
- *"宁德时代最新一期的利润表、资产负债表和前十大流通股东"*
- *"今天的龙虎榜，以及两市融资融券余额"*
- *"Give me 5-minute bars for 000001.SZ for today."*
- *"Which stocks are in the CSI 300 and what are their weights this month?"*
- *"List all ST stocks as of the last trading day and the upcoming restricted-share unlocks."*
- *"How have China's CPI, PPI and PMI moved over the past 24 months?"*
- *"Daily prices for Tencent and the top 10 southbound Stock Connect names today."*

## What's covered

**Listings and reference data**
- A-share list with code, name, listing and delisting dates; listed-company
  basics; management and their pay and holdings; historical name changes; ST
  and risk-warning lists by date; Beijing Stock Exchange old / new code mapping
- New IPO listings; daily suspensions and resumptions; daily limit-up and
  limit-down prices; pre-market share capital
- Hong Kong and US stock lists; trading calendars for the mainland, Hong Kong
  and US markets; Stock Connect (沪深港通) constituents

**Prices**
- A-share daily, weekly and monthly bars, raw and adjusted, with daily-updated
  weekly and monthly variants; adjustment factors; daily indicators for every
  stock
- Minute bars from 1 to 60 minutes for A-shares, Hong Kong stocks and futures;
  real-time minute data for A-shares; real-time daily candles for Hong Kong
  stocks and exchange indices
- Hong Kong daily and adjusted daily bars with share capital, market cap, turnover

**Financial statements and disclosures**
- Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and financial indicators for
  listed companies; main-business composition by region and by product
- Earnings forecasts (业绩预告), express reports (业绩快报), disclosure-date
  schedules, audit opinions, dividends and bonus shares

**Holders, flows and market reference**
- Top 10 shareholders and top 10 float shareholders, shareholder counts,
  major-holder increases and decreases, share pledges (statistics and detail),
  buybacks, restricted-share unlocks
- Block trades; Dragon-Tiger lists (龙虎榜) — daily statistics and
  institutional seat detail
- Margin trading summary and detail, eligible securities, securities-lending
  (转融通) summaries
- Stock Connect flows: northbound and southbound top 10 names, Hong Kong
  Connect daily and monthly turnover since 2014; weekly new brokerage accounts

**Indices and industry classification**
- Index basics, daily, weekly and monthly bars, monthly constituents and
  weights, daily indicators for the major benchmarks (SSE Composite, SZSE
  Component, SSE 50, CSI 500, SME Board, ChiNext), major global indices
- Shenwan (申万) industry classification in its 2014 and 2021 editions with
  members; CITIC (中信) industry indices and members; exchange daily trading
  statistics

**Funds, bonds and derivatives**
- ETFs: basics (including QDII), daily bars with more than ten years of
  history, adjustment factors, benchmark indices; mutual funds: list,
  managers, fund companies, NAV, dividends, quarterly holdings, size
- Convertible bonds: basics, daily bars, issuance, conversion-price changes,
  coupons, ratings, calls and redemptions, conversion results, top 10
  holders; bond repo daily, bond block trades, OTC bond quotes
- Futures: contracts, daily / weekly / monthly / minute bars, position
  rankings, main-contract mapping, settlement parameters, limit prices and
  margin rates since 2005, warehouse receipts, weekly exchange reports since
  2010; options contracts and daily bars; FX daily bars; Shanghai Gold
  Exchange spot contracts and daily prices

**China macro and rates**
- CPI (national, urban, rural), PPI, GDP, PMI, monthly money supply, monthly
  social financing, and the official data-release schedule
- Shibor and Shibor quotes, LPR, Hibor, Libor, Wenzhou and Guangzhou
  private-lending rates; US Treasury short- and long-term rates and yield
  curves

**Text corpora**
- Investor Q&A from the SSE e-互动 and SZSE 互动易 platforms; long-form
  financial news with more than eight years of history

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="贵州茅台最近一年的日线行情和复权因子")
find_tools(q="CSI 300 constituents and weights this month")
find_tools(prefix="finance/tushare")         # browse everything for China / HK
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Say "A-share", "港股" or "可转债" and the router lands on the right subtree.
Always `describe_tool` before executing — most tools take a Tushare code plus
a date range, and several cap rows per call, so long histories are pulled in
date windows.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which code format do the tools expect?">
    Tushare codes carry the exchange as a suffix — `600519.SH` for a Shanghai
    listing, `000001.SZ` for Shenzhen, `00700.HK` for Hong Kong — whereas the
    global quote routes use Yahoo-style suffixes such as `600519.SS` and
    `0700.HK`. Pull the stock list or check the example in `describe_tool`
    when converting between them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are prices adjusted for splits and dividends?">
    The daily bars include forward- and back-adjusted series alongside the raw
    close, and a separate adjustment-factor series covers the full history of
    a stock or a whole trading day, so you can adjust any bar yourself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to read Chinese?">
    No. Tool summaries are Chinese, but `find_tools(q=...)` matches an English
    request, and `describe_tool` returns the parameter list and a ready-to-run
    template. Chinese-language prompts work equally well.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
