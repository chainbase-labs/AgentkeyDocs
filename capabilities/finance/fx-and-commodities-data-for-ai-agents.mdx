Currency and commodity prices turn up in almost every analysis — converting a
revenue line, pricing an input cost, hedging a position — yet they come from
different vendors than equities and in different shapes. Through AgentKey your
agent gets a spot rate, a 20-year FX series or a Brent price history with the
same three calls — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — with
AlphaVantage behind the scenes for global FX, crypto and commodities, Tushare
for China FX, futures and Shanghai Gold, and Finnhub for symbol reference.

## What you can ask

- *"What is the EUR/USD rate right now?"*
- *"Monthly USD/JPY since 2000."*
- *"Daily Brent and WTI prices for the past year, side by side."*
- *"How has the Henry Hub natural gas price moved this quarter?"*
- *"Copper and aluminum prices over the last five years."*
- *"Wheat, corn, sugar and coffee — which has risen most this year?"*
- *"Gold and silver spot, and the Shanghai Gold Exchange's daily prices."*
- *"沪金现货和黄金期货主力合约最近一个月的走势"*

## What's covered

**Exchange rates**
- Realtime exchange rate for any pair of currencies, physical or digital
- Daily (20+ years), weekly and monthly series for a forex currency pair
- Daily FX bars and overseas FX instrument reference data on the China route
- Lists of supported forex exchanges and symbols

**Crypto series**
- Daily, weekly and monthly series for a digital currency on a given market
- Lists of supported crypto exchanges and symbols
- For on-chain, token and exchange data see the
  [Crypto](/capabilities/crypto) category

**Energy**
- West Texas Intermediate (WTI) and Brent crude oil prices
- Henry Hub natural gas spot prices

**Metals**
- Gold spot price and gold price history; silver spot price and history
- Global copper and aluminum prices
- Shanghai Gold Exchange spot contracts and their daily prices

**Agriculture and softs**
- Global wheat, corn, cotton, sugar and coffee prices
- The global price index of all commodities

**China futures and options**
- Futures contracts, daily / weekly / monthly / minute bars, position
  rankings, main- and continuous-contract mapping, settlement parameters,
  limit prices and margin rates, warehouse receipts; options contracts and
  daily bars — see [China A-shares](/capabilities/finance/china-a-share-and-hk-stock-data-for-ai-agents)

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="monthly USD/JPY exchange rate since 2000")
find_tools(q="最近一年布伦特原油价格")
find_tools(prefix="finance/alphavantage")    # FX, crypto and commodity series
find_tools(prefix="finance/tushare")         # China FX, futures, Shanghai Gold
find_tools(prefix="finance/finnhub")         # forex and crypto symbol lists
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Name the pair or the commodity and the interval you want. Always
`describe_tool` before executing — currency-code and interval parameters and
per-call costs differ between providers.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** There is no dedicated Scenario Method for FX or
  commodities yet; the capability is available through the catalog today. See
  the [Scenario Methods overview](/api-reference/scenario/overview) for the
  fixed-contract surface that does exist.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the exchange rate live?">
    The currency exchange rate tool returns a realtime rate for the pair you
    name, including crypto pairs. The FX, crypto and commodity time series are
    periodic — daily, weekly or monthly — and `describe_tool` lists the
    intervals each one supports.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much FX history is there?">
    Daily FX series reach back 20+ years on the AlphaVantage route. For the
    commodity series and the China routes, `describe_tool` documents the
    range and any row limits per call.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do crypto prices live — here or under Crypto?">
    Here you get exchange-style OHLC series for a digital currency on a named
    market, alongside FX. Token metadata, on-chain holders, wallets and
    derivatives live under the Crypto category.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
