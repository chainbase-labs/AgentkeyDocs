Price data is the first thing a finance agent needs and one of the most
fragmented: every vendor has its own symbols, intervals and limits, and the
exchange feeds themselves are priced for institutions. Through AgentKey your
agent pulls quotes, candles, option chains and market status the same way it
pulls everything else — `find_tools`, `describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — with
yfinance, Finnhub and AlphaVantage behind the scenes for global markets and
Tushare for China and Hong Kong.

## What you can ask

- *"What is NVDA trading at right now, and how far is it from its 52-week high?"*
- *"Give me two years of daily OHLCV for AAPL so I can backtest a moving-average crossover."*
- *"Weekly candles for Microsoft since 2005, adjusted for dividends."*
- *"Find the ticker for 'Taiwan Semiconductor' and tell me which exchanges it trades on."*
- *"Which tickers are trending in the US today, and who are the biggest gainers and losers?"*
- *"Pull the TSLA option chain for next month's expiry with implied volatility."*
- *"Are the Tokyo and London exchanges open right now?"*
- *"Compute the 14-day RSI and Bollinger Bands for MSFT."*

## What's covered

**Quotes**
- Snapshot quote for one or several symbols: last price, change and percent
  change, day range, volume, market cap, P/E and 52-week high/low — for
  stocks, ETFs, indices, FX and crypto
- Real-time quotes for US stocks
- Latest price and volume for a single ticker
- Top gainers, losers and most actively traded tickers in the US market;
  trending tickers by region

**Candles and time series**
- OHLCV candles over a date range at daily, weekly, monthly or intraday
  intervals — stocks, ETFs, indices, FX and crypto
- Raw and adjusted daily, weekly and monthly series (adjusted close, dividends)
  covering 20+ years of history
- A-share, Hong Kong and index bars down to one-minute resolution — see
  [China A-shares](/capabilities/finance/china-a-shares)

**Symbols and screening**
- Ticker search by company name, keyword, ISIN or CUSIP; as-you-type
  autocomplete
- Lists of supported stocks per exchange; active or delisted US stocks and ETFs
  as of a date
- Screener: rank stocks, ETFs, mutual funds and crypto by market cap, price,
  percent change, sector or region

**Options**
- Option chains: calls and puts across strikes and expirations with bid/ask,
  last price, volume, open interest and implied volatility
- Realtime and historical US equity options data; realtime put-call ratio

**Market status**
- Whether the major equity, forex and crypto venues around the world are
  currently open or closed
- Holiday calendars for global exchanges

**Technical indicators and analytics**
- Moving averages (SMA, EMA, WMA, DEMA, TEMA, TRIMA, KAMA, MAMA, T3), MACD,
  RSI, Stochastics, Bollinger Bands, ADX / DMI, Aroon, ATR, CCI, MFI, OBV,
  Chaikin A/D, Parabolic SAR, Williams %R and Hilbert Transform cycle studies
- Analytics computed over a fixed or sliding window for one or more symbols

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="two years of daily OHLCV for AAPL")
find_tools(q="特斯拉现在的股价")
find_tools(prefix="finance/yfinance")        # quotes, charts, option chain, screener
find_tools(prefix="finance/finnhub")         # real-time US quotes, symbol lookup, market status
find_tools(prefix="finance/alphavantage")    # 20-year series, options, technical indicators
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

Name the interval and the market in the request ("weekly", "Hong Kong",
"options") and the router ranks the right provider first. Always
`describe_tool` before executing — symbol formats, interval names and per-call
costs differ between providers.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are quotes real-time or delayed?">
    It depends on the route. One provider serves real-time quotes for US
    stocks; another returns a real-time or delayed snapshot depending on the
    exchange. `describe_tool` states what each tool returns. Historical candles
    are the same on every route.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which symbol format should I use?">
    Most routes take the exchange-suffixed form, for example `0700.HK` for
    Tencent in Hong Kong or `600519.SS` for Kweichow Moutai in Shanghai, while
    plain `AAPL` works for US listings. One provider lists its symbols as
    exchange ticker plus exchange code. When unsure, resolve the name with the
    ticker search first and pass the symbol it returns.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much history is available?">
    Daily, weekly and monthly series reach back 20+ years on the time-series
    routes. Intraday bars cover shorter windows; `describe_tool` documents the
    range each interval supports.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
