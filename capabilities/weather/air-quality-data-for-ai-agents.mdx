Air quality decides whether to run outside, open a window or move an event
indoors, and the right number depends on where you stand — the index used in
Beijing is not the one used in Bangkok. Through AgentKey your agent reads
QWeather air-quality data with the same three calls — `find_tools`,
`describe_tool`, `execute_tool` — for current conditions, hourly and daily
forecasts, and the raw readings of individual monitoring stations.

## What you can ask

- *"What's the air quality in Beijing right now, and what's the main pollutant?"*
- *"Will PM2.5 in Delhi be worse this afternoon?"*
- *"上海未来三天空气质量预报怎么样？"*
- *"Is it safe to run outdoors in Bangkok tomorrow morning?"*
- *"Give me the hourly AQI for the next 24 hours at this coordinate."*
- *"Which monitoring station is nearest to this point, and what does it read?"*
- *"Compare today's air quality in Seoul using the local standard and a common index."*

## What's covered

**Current air quality**
- Real-time air quality for a latitude and longitude at 1 km resolution
- The AQI, its level, colour and primary pollutant under the local standard
  of the country or region, plus QWeather's universal AQI for comparison
  across borders
- Pollutant concentrations and per-pollutant sub-indices
- Health advice, and the monitoring stations the reading is associated with

**Forecasts**
- Hourly air quality for the next 24 hours: AQI, concentrations, sub-indices
  and health advice
- Daily air quality for the next three days: AQI, concentrations and health
  advice

**Monitoring stations**
- Pollutant concentrations as measured at a specific station, by station id
- Station readings are observational and can be delayed or missing because
  of maintenance, removal or local regulation

## How to use it

Pass the request in plain language and let the router pick the tool:

```text
find_tools(q="air quality in Beijing right now with main pollutant")
find_tools(q="上海未来三天空气质量")
find_tools(prefix="weather/qweather")      # browse all weather tools
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { latitude: 39.9, longitude: 116.4, ...params })
```

Air-quality tools take a latitude and longitude; use
[location lookup](/capabilities/weather/weather-location-lookup-for-ai-agents) to turn a place name
into coordinates, and take station ids from the current-air-quality response.
Always `describe_tool` before executing.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** Air quality is available through the catalog tools
  above; there is no fixed-contract
  [Scenario Method](/api-reference/scenario/overview) for it yet. The daily
  weather forecast and place lookup do have methods — see
  [Forecasts](/capabilities/weather/weather-forecasts-for-ai-agents).
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which AQI standard is used?">
    Both. Each reading reports the index defined by the local authority of
    the country or region, and QWeather's universal AQI so that two cities can
    be compared on one scale.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which countries are covered?">
    The local-standard AQI is available for the countries and regions
    QWeather supports; the universal index and pollutant concentrations are
    returned for any coordinate it has data for. Health advice and names can
    be localized through the `lang` parameter.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fresh is the data?">
    Fetched at request time. Current readings are near-real-time; the hourly
    and daily forecasts reflect the latest run available when you call.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
