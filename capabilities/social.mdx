The **Social** category is AgentKey's largest: thousands of tools covering posts,
profiles, comments, followers, search and trending feeds on dozens of
platforms. It is what an agent uses to monitor a brand, measure a trend, pull a
creator's history or read what a community is saying — without holding
credentials for each platform.

## Platforms

Tools come from several providers — **TikHub**, **JustOneAPI**, **RedFox**,
**Rnote**, **Sorsa** and **AnyAPI** — and most platforms are served by more
than one of them, so when one route fails you can pick another from the same `find_tools`
result. Global platforms are listed first; the Chinese ecosystem, which is
unusually deep in this catalog, follows. Each platform has its own page with
example prompts and a full capability list.

<Note>
  **Building with code?** Developers and enterprise integrations can call this

## Browse the catalog

```text
find_tools(prefix="social")                   # the whole category
find_tools(prefix="social/twitter")           # one platform's subtree
find_tools(q="what people are saying about AgentKey on X today")
find_tools(q="小红书上关于防晒霜的热门笔记")        # Chinese works too
describe_tool("<returned Provider/Operation>")
execute_tool("<Provider/Operation>", { ...params })
```

<Note>
  Endpoints, parameters and per-call costs differ widely across platforms and
  providers — always `describe_tool` before executing. `execute_tool` calls
  exactly the tool you name; if you want AgentKey to pick the route and fail
  over for you, use a Scenario Method.
</Note>
