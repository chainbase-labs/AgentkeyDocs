AgentKey bills through a **subscription**. Each plan includes a monthly **credit
allowance** shared across every service, every surface and every agent you use.
Nothing is billed automatically beyond your plan: when the allowance runs out you
buy extra credits or move up a plan.

## Plans

| Plan | Who it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** | Trying AgentKey. No card needed, and it never converts to a paid plan on its own. |
| **Lite** | Regular, hands-on use. |
| **Pro** | Daily, serious work. Priority support and higher concurrency limits. |
| **Max** | Heavy, production workloads. Dedicated support, highest concurrency and an SLA. |

Included credits, prices and the annual discount are on the
[pricing page](https://agentkey.app/pricing.html). You can upgrade, downgrade or
cancel anytime from the [console](https://console.agentkey.app).

## Credits

A credit is the unit AgentKey uses to measure usage. Different tools consume
different amounts depending on the provider, the data source and the request:

- **Catalog tools** show their per-call cost in `find_tools` and `describe_tool`.
  Metered tools (for example page scraping) quote a minimum; the execute result
  reports the actual `credits_charged`.
- **Scenario Methods** have one fixed price each, shown in the catalog and in
  describe, charged once per successful call whichever provider served it.

Calls draw down the monthly allowance first, then any extra credits.

## When credits run out

- Monthly credits reset each billing cycle.
- On a paid plan you can buy **extra credits** — one-time add-ons that outlast
  the monthly allowance — or upgrade to a bigger plan.
- On the Free plan, extra credits are not available; upgrade to keep going.
- Once both the allowance and extra credits are used up, calls return
  `402 insufficient_credits` with a link to the console. Nothing is charged for
  those calls, and your agent is told how to continue.

## See the cost before you spend

Every `find_tools` and `describe_tool` result includes the per-call cost in
credits, and every Scenario Method lists its fixed price. Before any bulk work,
multiply the cost by your planned call count and check your remaining credits
with the free [`agentkey_account`](/api-reference/account) tool.

<Tip>
  For work involving several calls or a meaningful credit estimate, confirm the
  plan and cost with the user before proceeding.
</Tip>

## Managing your plan

Choose or change your plan, buy extra credits and review usage from the
[console](https://console.agentkey.app). The `agentkey_account` tool returns
your plan, remaining allowance, extra credits and upstream health at any time —
and never costs credits to call.
